# Area of Triangle Example

**Problem:**
The lengths of the sides of a triangular field are 50 m, 78 m, and 112 m. Find the cost of leveling the field at the rate of ₹ 6.50 per square meter.

(A) ₹ 10920
(B) ₹ 11700
(C) ₹ 12480
(D) ₹ 9845

## Solution:

This problem involves a two-step process: first calculating the area of a triangle when all three sides are known, and second, calculating the total cost based on that area. We need to use Heron's formula for the area calculation, as outlined in [[14 - Mensuration (2D and 3D)/01 - 2D Shapes Area and Perimeter (Triangle, Square, Rectangle, Circle).md|the concept note covering triangle area formulas]].

**Step 1: Identify Given Information and Goal**
Given: Triangle sides `a = 50` m, `b = 78` m, `c = 112` m. Leveling rate = ₹ 6.50 per m².
Goal: Find the total cost of leveling the field.

**Step 2: Calculate the Semi-perimeter (s)**
Heron's formula requires the semi-perimeter (`s`).
`s = (a + b + c) / 2`
`s = (50 + 78 + 112) / 2`
`s = 240 / 2`
`s = 120` m

**Step 3: Apply Heron's Formula to Find the Area (A)**
The formula is `A = sqrt(s * (s - a) * (s - b) * (s - c))`.
Substitute the values:
`A = sqrt(120 * (120 - 50) * (120 - 78) * (120 - 112))`
`A = sqrt(120 * 70 * 42 * 8)`

**Step 4: Simplify the Square Root**
To simplify `sqrt(120 * 70 * 42 * 8)`, factorize the numbers:
120 = 12 * 10 = (2² * 3) * (2 * 5) = 2³ * 3 * 5
70 = 7 * 10 = 7 * 2 * 5
42 = 6 * 7 = (2 * 3) * 7
8 = 2³
Combine the factors:
`A = sqrt((2³ * 3 * 5) * (2 * 5 * 7) * (2 * 3 * 7) * (2³))`
`A = sqrt(2^(3+1+1+3) * 3^(1+1) * 5^(1+1) * 7^(1+1))`
`A = sqrt(2^8 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7^2)`
Take the square root by halving the exponents:
`A = 2^(8/2) * 3^(2/2) * 5^(2/2) * 7^(2/2)`
`A = 2^4 * 3^1 * 5^1 * 7^1`
`A = 16 * 3 * 5 * 7`
`A = 16 * 105`
`A = 1680` square meters

**Step 5: Calculate the Total Cost**
Cost = Area * Rate per square meter
Cost = 1680 * 6.50
Cost = 1680 * (13 / 2)  *(converting 6.50 to fraction for easier calculation)*
Cost = (1680 / 2) * 13
Cost = 840 * 13
Cost = 10920

**Step 6: State the Final Cost**
The total cost of leveling the field is ₹ 10920. This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Recognize Heron's:** If a triangle area problem gives 3 side lengths, Heron's formula is the way to go.
2.  **Factorize Early:** Don't multiply `120 * 70 * 42 * 8` first. Factorize *within* the square root expression (`sqrt((...) * (...) * (...) * (...))`). This makes finding pairs or powers for the square root much simpler and less error-prone.
3.  **Simplify Multiplication:** When calculating `16 * 105`, you could do `16 * (100 + 5) = 1600 + 80 = 1680`. For `840 * 13`, you could do `840 * (10 + 3) = 8400 + (840 * 3) = 8400 + 2520 = 10920`. Breaking down multiplications can speed things up.
4.  **Handle Decimals:** Convert rates like 6.50 to fractions (13/2) if it simplifies multiplication or division, especially with even numbers like 1680.
5.  **Units:** Check units carefully. Sides in 'm', Area in 'm²', Rate in '₹ per m²'. The final cost will be in '₹'.
