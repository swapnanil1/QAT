# Surface Area of Cylinder Example

**Problem:**
The volume of a right circular cylinder is 1540 cm³. If the radius of its base is 7 cm, find the cost of polishing its Total Surface Area (TSA) at the rate of ₹ 2 per cm². (Use `pi = 22/7`)

(A) ₹ 748
(B) ₹ 1496
(C) ₹ 880
(D) ₹ 1540

## Solution:

This problem requires finding the height of the cylinder using the given volume and radius, then calculating the Total Surface Area (TSA), and finally determining the polishing cost. The relevant cylinder formulas are found in [[14 - Mensuration (2D and 3D)/02 - 3D Shapes Surface Area and Volume (Cube, Cuboid, Cylinder, Cone, Sphere).md|the concept note on 3D shapes]].

**Step 1: Identify Given Information and Goal**
Given: Volume (V) = 1540 cm³, Radius (r) = 7 cm, Rate = ₹ 2 / cm². Use `pi = 22/7`.
Goal: Find the total cost to polish the Total Surface Area (TSA).

**Step 2: Find the Height (h) using the Volume Formula**
The formula for the volume of a cylinder is `V = pi * r^2 * h`.
Substitute the given values:
1540 = (22/7) * (7)^2 * `h`
1540 = (22/7) * 49 * `h`
Simplify the calculation: 49 / 7 = 7.
1540 = 22 * 7 * `h`
1540 = 154 * `h`
Solve for `h`:
`h = 1540 / 154`
`h = 10` cm

**Step 3: Calculate the Total Surface Area (TSA)**
The formula for TSA of a cylinder is `TSA = 2 * pi * r * (h + r)`.
Substitute the known dimensions (`r=7`, `h=10`) and `pi = 22/7`:
`TSA = 2 * (22/7) * 7 * (10 + 7)`
`TSA = 2 * 22 * (17)`
`TSA = 44 * 17`
Calculate the product: 44 * 17 = 44 * (10 + 7) = 440 + 308 = 748.
`TSA = 748` cm²

**Step 4: Calculate the Total Cost**
Cost = Total Surface Area * Rate per cm²
Cost = 748 * 2
Cost = 1496

**Step 5: State the Final Cost**
The total cost to polish the TSA of the cylinder is ₹ 1496. This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Multi-Step Planning:** Recognize the sequence: Use Volume to find `h` -> Use `r` and `h` to find TSA -> Use TSA to find Cost.
2.  **Formula Choice:** Be sure to use the **Total Surface Area** formula (`TSA = 2 * pi * r * (h + r)`) including the top and bottom circles, not just the Curved Surface Area (`CSA = 2 * pi * r * h`). Using CSA would lead to CSA = 2 * (22/7) * 7 * 10 = 440 cm², Cost = 440 * 2 = ₹ 880 (Option C, a distractor).
3.  **Simplify Calculations:** Cancel terms early, like `(49/7 = 7)` in the volume calculation and `(7/7 = 1)` in the TSA calculation.
4.  **Check Intermediate Results:** Does `h=10` seem reasonable given `r=7` and `V=1540`? Yes. Does `TSA=748` seem reasonable? Yes.
5.  **Distractor Awareness:** Option (A) is the TSA value itself, forgetting the cost step. Option (C) is the cost if only CSA was polished. Option (D) uses the volume value (1540) mistakenly in the cost calculation. Being aware of these common errors helps avoid them.
