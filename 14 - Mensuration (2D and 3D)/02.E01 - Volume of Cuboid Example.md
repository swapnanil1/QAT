# Volume of Cuboid Example

**Problem:**
The volume of a cuboid is 720 cm³. If its length is 10 cm and breadth is 8 cm, find the cost of painting its Total Surface Area (TSA) at a rate of ₹ 0.50 per cm².

(A) ₹ 242
(B) ₹ 360
(C) ₹ 484
(D) ₹ 162

## Solution:

This problem requires finding the missing dimension (height) using the volume formula, then calculating the Total Surface Area, and finally computing the cost. The necessary formulas are detailed in [[14 - Mensuration (2D and 3D)/02 - 3D Shapes Surface Area and Volume (Cube, Cuboid, Cylinder, Cone, Sphere).md|the concept note on 3D shapes]].

**Step 1: Identify Given Information and Goal**
Given: Volume (V) = 720 cm³, Length (l) = 10 cm, Breadth (b) = 8 cm, Rate = ₹ 0.50 / cm².
Goal: Find the total cost to paint the Total Surface Area (TSA).

**Step 2: Find the Height (h) using the Volume Formula**
The formula for the volume of a cuboid is `V = l * b * h`.
Substitute the given values:
720 = 10 * 8 * `h`
720 = 80 * `h`
Solve for `h`:
`h = 720 / 80`
`h = 9` cm

**Step 3: Calculate the Total Surface Area (TSA)**
The formula for TSA of a cuboid is `TSA = 2 * (lb + bh + lh)`.
Substitute the known dimensions (`l=10`, `b=8`, `h=9`):
`TSA = 2 * ( (10 * 8) + (8 * 9) + (10 * 9) )`
`TSA = 2 * ( 80 + 72 + 90 )`
`TSA = 2 * ( 242 )`
`TSA = 484` cm²

**Step 4: Calculate the Total Cost**
Cost = Total Surface Area * Rate per cm²
Cost = 484 * 0.50
Cost = 484 * (1/2)
Cost = 242

**Step 5: State the Final Cost**
The total cost to paint the TSA of the cuboid is ₹ 242. This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Structured Approach:** Break multi-step problems down: Find missing dimension -> Calculate required area -> Calculate cost.
2.  **Formula Accuracy:** Ensure you use the correct formula for **Total** Surface Area (`TSA = 2*(lb+bh+lh)`), not Lateral Surface Area (`LSA = 2*(l+b)*h`). Using LSA here would lead to LSA = 2*(10+8)*9 = 324 cm², Cost = 324 * 0.5 = ₹ 162 (Option D, a common distractor).
3.  **Calculation with Decimals:** When multiplying by 0.50, it's often quicker to recognize this is the same as dividing by 2.
4.  **Units Check:** Volume (cm³), Dimensions (cm), Rate (₹/cm²), TSA (cm²), Cost (₹). Ensure consistency throughout.
5.  **Distractor Analysis:** Option (B) ₹ 360 might come from mistakenly multiplying the Volume (720) by the rate (0.5). Option (C) ₹ 484 is the TSA value itself, forgetting the final cost calculation step. Option (D) ₹ 162 is the cost if only LSA was painted. Recognizing potential errors helps confirm the correct answer.
