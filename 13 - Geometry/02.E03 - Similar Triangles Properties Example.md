# Similar Triangles Properties Example

**Problem:**
In the figure below, angle ABC is equal to angle ADE, and angle BAC is equal to angle DAE (common angle A). If AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm, and DE = 5 cm, find the length of BC.

**(Imagine a triangle ADE, with point B on side AD extended, and point C on side AE extended, such that line segment BC is parallel to DE *or* positioned such that Angle ABC = Angle ADE. The problem statement clarifies it's the latter angle equality.)**

*Correction:* The problem states Angle ABC = Angle ADE. B must be on segment AD, and E must be on segment AC for standard similar triangle setups like this, or D on AB and E on AC. Let's assume D is on AB and E is on AC.
**(Corrected mental image: Triangle ABC. Point D is on side AB, point E is on side AC. Line segment DE connects D and E. It is given that Angle ADE = Angle ABC.)**

Given: AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm, DE = 5 cm. Angle ADE = Angle ABC. Find BC.

(A) 7.5 cm
(B) 10 cm
(C) 12.5 cm
(D) 15 cm

## Solution:

This problem involves similar triangles. We need to prove similarity and then use the property that corresponding sides are proportional, as discussed in [[13 - Geometry/02 - Triangles (Properties, Congruence, Similarity).md]].

**Step 1: Identify the Triangles**
We have two triangles to consider: Triangle ADE and Triangle ABC.

**Step 2: Prove Similarity**
We are given:
1.  Angle ADE = Angle ABC (Given)
2.  Angle DAE = Angle BAC (This is the common angle A for both triangles)

Since two angles of Triangle ADE are equal to two corresponding angles of Triangle ABC, the triangles are similar by the **AA (Angle-Angle) similarity criterion**.
Therefore, `△ADE ~ △ABC`.

**Step 3: Identify Corresponding Sides**
The similarity statement `△ADE ~ △ABC` helps identify corresponding vertices and hence sides:
*   A corresponds to A
*   D corresponds to B
*   E corresponds to C

Corresponding sides are opposite corresponding angles:
*   AD corresponds to AB
*   AE corresponds to AC
*   DE corresponds to BC

**Step 4: Calculate Full Side Lengths**
We are given AD = 4 cm and DB = 6 cm.
Therefore, the length of side AB = AD + DB = 4 + 6 = 10 cm.
We are given DE = 5 cm.
We need to find BC.

**Step 5: Set up the Proportion**
Since the triangles are similar, the ratio of corresponding sides is equal:
`AD / AB = AE / AC = DE / BC`

We need the sides involving AD, AB, DE, and BC:
`AD / AB = DE / BC`

**Step 6: Substitute Known Values and Solve for BC**
Substitute the lengths we know:
`4 / 10 = 5 / BC`

Cross-multiply to solve for BC:
`4 * BC = 10 * 5`
`4 * BC = 50`
`BC = 50 / 4`
`BC = 25 / 2`
`BC = 12.5` cm

The length of BC is 12.5 cm. This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Establish Similarity:** The first step is always proving similarity. AA (Angle-Angle) is the most common and quickest method if applicable. Look for shared angles and given equal angles or parallel lines (which create equal corresponding or alternate interior angles, see [[13 - Geometry/01 - Lines and Angles.md]]).
2.  **Correct Correspondence:** Writing the similarity statement (`△ADE ~ △ABC`) correctly is crucial for matching corresponding sides. A corresponds to A, D to B, E to C.
3.  **Set up Proportion Carefully:** Ensure you are comparing corresponding sides: `(Small Triangle Side) / (Large Triangle Side) = (Small Triangle Side) / (Large Triangle Side)`. Here: `AD/AB = DE/BC`. A common mistake is mismatching sides, e.g., writing `AD/DB = DE/BC`.
4.  **Full Lengths:** Remember to use the full side length of the larger triangle (AB = AD + DB) when setting up the proportion.
5.  **Ratio Check:** The ratio of sides is `AD/AB = 4/10 = 2/5`. The ratio of `DE/BC` must also be `2/5`. `5 / BC = 2 / 5` => `2 * BC = 25` => `BC = 12.5`.
