**Problem:**

A certain sum of money amounts to Rs. 1008 in 2 years and to Rs. 1164 in 3.5 years at simple interest. Find the sum (Principal) and the rate of interest.
**Options:**
(A) Principal = Rs. 800, Rate = 12%
(B) Principal = Rs. 852, Rate = 13%
(C) Principal = Rs. 800, Rate = 13%
(D) Principal = Rs. 850, Rate = 12.5%

**Solution:**

This problem requires finding the SI earned over a specific period by looking at the difference in amounts, and then working backwards to find the Principal and Rate.

**1. Understand the given information:**
*   Amount after 2 years (A2) = Principal (P) + SI for 2 years = Rs. 1008
*   Amount after 3.5 years (A3.5) = Principal (P) + SI for 3.5 years = Rs. 1164

**2. Calculate the Simple Interest earned between Year 2 and Year 3.5:**

The difference between the amounts represents the Simple Interest earned during the time interval between the two measurements.
*   Time interval = 3.5 years - 2 years = 1.5 years
*   SI earned in 1.5 years = A3.5 - A2 = Rs. 1164 - Rs. 1008 = Rs. 156

**3. Calculate the Simple Interest per year:**

Since Simple Interest is constant each year (assuming constant P and R):
*   SI for 1 year = SI for 1.5 years / 1.5
*   SI for 1 year = 156 / 1.5 = 156 / (3/2) = 156 * (2/3) = 52 * 2 = Rs. 104

**4. Calculate the Principal (P):**

We know the Amount after 2 years (A2) and the SI per year.
*   SI for 2 years = SI per year * 2 = 104 * 2 = Rs. 208
*   We also know that A2 = P + SI for 2 years
*   1008 = P + 208
*   P = 1008 - 208
*   P = Rs. 800

**5. Calculate the Rate of Interest (R):**

Now that we have the Principal (P = 800) and the SI for 1 year (SI = 104 when T = 1), we can use the Rate formula: ```R = (SI * 100) / (P * T)```
`Using SI for 1 year:`
    R = (104 * 100) / (800 * 1)  
	R = 10400 / 800  
	R = 104 / 8  
	R = 13
So, the rate of interest is 13% per annum.

**Answer:**

*   The Principal (Sum) = Rs. 800
*   The Rate of Interest = 13% p.a.
