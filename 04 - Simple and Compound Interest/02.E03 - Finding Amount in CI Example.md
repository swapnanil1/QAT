**Problem:**

Find the amount on a principal of Rs. 15,000 invested for 2 years and 4 months at an annual interest rate of 8%, compounded annually.
**Options:**
(A) Rs. 17800.00
(B) Rs. 18895.68
(C) Rs. 17496.00
(D) Rs. 17962.56

**Solution:**

This problem involves calculating the final amount using compound interest where the time period includes a fraction of a year.

**1. Identify the variables:**
*   Principal (P) = Rs. 15,000
*   Annual Rate (R) = 8% p.a.
*   Time (T) = 2 years and 4 months.
*   Compounding is done annually.

**2. Convert Time to the required format:**
The time period is 2 full years and a fraction of the next year.
*   Fractional part = 4 months = 4/12 year = 1/3 year.
*   So, the time can be represented as `T = a + b/c` years, where `a = 2` (whole years) and `b/c = 1/3` (fractional year).

**3. Apply the formula for fractional time periods (compounded annually):**

When time T = a (b/c) years, the Amount A is calculated as:
`A = P * (1 + R/100)^a * (1 + ((b/c) * R)/100)`

This formula calculates the compound amount for the whole number of years ('a') and then applies simple interest for the fractional part ('b/c') of the year on the amount accumulated till then.

**4. Substitute the values:**

Here, P = 15000, R = 8, a = 2, b/c = 1/3.

A = 15000 * (1 + 8/100)^2 * (1 + ((1/3) * 8)/100)
A = 15000 * (1 + 2/25)^2 * (1 + (8/3)/100)
A = 15000 * (27/25)^2 * (1 + 8/300)
A = 15000 * (27/25) * (27/25) * (1 + 2/75)
A = 15000 * (729 / 625) * (77/75)

**5. Calculate the Amount (A):**

Simplify the calculation step-by-step:

A = (15000 / 75) * (729 / 625) * 77
A = 200 * (729 / 625) * 77
Divide 200 and 625 by 25:
A = (8 * 729 * 77) / 25
A = (5832 * 77) / 25
A = 449064 / 25
A = 17962.56

So, A = Rs. 17,962.56

**Answer:**

The amount after 2 years and 4 months is Rs. 17,962.56.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Estimation for MCQs:** Calculate the approximate amount after the whole number of years (2 years) and slightly more.
    *   Amount after 2 years `A_2 = P*(1+R/100)^2`. Here, `A_2 = 15000 * (1.08)^2 = 15000 * 1.1664 ≈ 17496`.
    *   Simple Interest for the next 4 months (1/3 year) on A2 ≈ `(A_2 * R * (1/3)) / 100`. Approx: `(17496 * 8 * (1/3)) / 100 ≈ (17500 * 8) / 300 = 1400 / 3 ≈ 467`.
    *   Estimated Final Amount ≈ 17496 + 467 ≈ 17963. This is very close to the actual answer and helps eliminate options significantly different.
*   **Bounding:** The final amount *must* be between the amount compounded for 2 years and the amount compounded for 3 years.
    *   A(2 years) ≈ 17496
    *   A(3 years) = A(2 years) * 1.08 ≈ 17496 * 1.08 ≈ 18895
    *   The answer must lie between ~17500 and ~18900. Use this to check options in MCQs.
*   **Calculation Simplification:** Look for common factors early. `A = 15000 * (27/25)^2 * (77/75)`. Notice 15000 is divisible by factors in the denominators. Dividing `15000 / 75 = 200` and `625 = 25 * 25`. The expression becomes `A = 200 * (729/625) * 77`. Then simplify `200/625` by dividing both by 25 to get `8/25`. So `A = (8 * 729 * 77) / 25`. Multiplying by 4/4 to get denominator 100 often makes the final division easier: `A = (449064 * 4) / (25 * 4) = 1796256 / 100 = 17962.56`.
