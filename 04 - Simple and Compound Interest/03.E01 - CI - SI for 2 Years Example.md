**Problem:**

The difference between the compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum of money at 10% per annum for 2 years is Rs. 40. Find the sum (Principal).

**Options:**
(A) Rs. 3000
(B) Rs. 4000
(C) Rs. 3500
(D) Rs. 4500

**Solution:**

This problem uses the direct formula for the difference between CI and SI for a period of 2 years.

**1. Identify the given information:**
*   Difference (CI - SI) = Rs. 40
*   Rate (R) = 10% p.a.
*   Time (T) = 2 years
*   Principal (P) = ? (This needs to be found)

**2. Recall the formula for the difference for 2 years:**

The difference between CI and SI for 2 years is given by:
`CI - SI (for 2 years) = P * (R / 100)^2`

**3. Substitute the known values into the formula:**

40 = P * (10 / 100)^2
40 = P * (1 / 10)^2
40 = P * (1 / 100)

**4. Solve for P:**

Multiply both sides by 100:
P = 40 * 100
P = 4000

**Answer:**

The sum (Principal) is Rs. 4000.
Correct Option: **(B)**

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Formula Application:** This type of question is a direct application of the formula `Diff = P * (R/100)^2`. Memorizing this saves significant time compared to calculating CI and SI separately and then finding the difference.
*   **Working Backwards from Options (MCQ Strategy):** If unsure about the formula, you can test the options. Let's test option (B) P=4000:
    *   SI for 2 years = (4000 * 10 * 2) / 100 = 800
    *   CI for 2 years:
        *   Year 1 Interest = (4000 * 10 * 1) / 100 = 400
        *   Amount after 1 year = 4000 + 400 = 4400
        *   Year 2 Interest = (4400 * 10 * 1) / 100 = 440
        *   Total CI = 400 + 440 = 840
    *   Difference = CI - SI = 840 - 800 = 40. This matches the problem statement, confirming P=4000. This takes longer but is a valid checking method.
*   **Estimation:** The difference (40) is `(R/100)^2 = (1/10)^2 = 1/100` times the principal. So, Principal = Difference * 100 = 40 * 100 = 4000.
