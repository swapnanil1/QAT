**Problem:**

Find the compound interest on Rs. 16,000 for 2 years, compounded annually, if the rate of interest is 10% for the first year and 12% for the second year.
**Options:**
(A) Rs. 3520
(B) Rs. 3713.60
(C) Rs. 3360
(D) Rs. 3712

**Solution:**

This problem involves calculating compound interest when the interest rate changes each year.

**1. Identify the variables:**
*   Principal (P) = Rs. 16,000
*   Time = 2 years
*   Rate for the 1st year (R1) = 10% p.a.
*   Rate for the 2nd year (R2) = 12% p.a.
*   Compounding is done annually.

**2. Apply the formula for variable rates (compounded annually):**

When the rates are different for different years (R1, R2, ...), the Amount (A) is calculated as:
`A = P * (1 + R1/100) * (1 + R2/100) * ...`

**3. Substitute the values:**

For 2 years with rates R1 and R2:
A = 16000 * (1 + 10/100) * (1 + 12/100)
A = 16000 * (1 + 0.10) * (1 + 0.12)
A = 16000 * (1.1) * (1.12)

**4. Calculate the Amount (A):**

A = 16000 * 1.1 * 1.12
A = 17600 * 1.12
A = 19712

So, A = Rs. 19,712

**5. Calculate the Compound Interest (CI):**

Compound Interest is the difference between the final Amount and the initial Principal.
`CI = A - P`
CI = 19712 - 16000
CI = Rs. 3,712

**Alternative Step-by-Step Calculation (Conceptual):**

*   **Interest for Year 1:**
    SI1 = (16000 * 10 * 1) / 100 = Rs. 1600
    Amount after Year 1 = P + SI1 = 16000 + 1600 = Rs. 17600
*   **Interest for Year 2:** (Calculated on the amount after Year 1, using R2)
    Principal for Year 2 = Rs. 17600
    Rate for Year 2 = 12%
    SI2 = (17600 * 12 * 1) / 100 = 176 * 12 = Rs. 2112
    Amount after Year 2 = Principal for Year 2 + SI2 = 17600 + 2112 = Rs. 19712
*   **Total Compound Interest:**
    Total CI = SI1 + SI2 = 1600 + 2112 = Rs. 3712
    Or, Total CI = Final Amount - Initial Principal = 19712 - 16000 = Rs. 3712

**Answer:**

The compound interest is Rs. 3,712.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Mental Math/Approximation:** Calculate Year 1 interest (10% of 16k = 1600). New principal ≈ 17600. Calculate Year 2 interest (12% of 17600 ≈ 10% of 17600 + 2% of 17600 ≈ 1760 + 352 ≈ 2112). Total CI ≈ 1600 + 2112 = 3712. This confirms the calculation quickly.
*   **Avoid Common Mistake:** Do not average the rates (10% and 12% -> average 11%) and apply it for 2 years using the standard CI formula `P*(1+R/100)^T`. This will give an incorrect answer because the base for the second year's interest calculation is different in the variable rate scenario.
    *   Incorrect method: `16000 * (1.11)^2 = 16000 * 1.2321 = 19713.6`. (Close, but not exact). CI = 3713.6 (Wrong).
