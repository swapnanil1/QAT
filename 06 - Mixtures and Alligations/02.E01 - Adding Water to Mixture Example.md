## Problem:

A 60-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. How many litres of water must be added to this mixture so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 3 : 7?

(A) 40 litres
(B) 60 litres
(C) 80 litres
(D) 70 litres

## Solution:

**1. Calculate Initial Quantities:**
   Total mixture = 60 litres.
   Ratio of Milk : Water = 7 : 3.
   Sum of ratio parts = 7 + 3 = 10 parts.
   Value of 1 part = 60 litres / 10 parts = 6 litres/part.

   Initial quantity of Milk = 7 parts * 6 litres/part = 42 litres.
   Initial quantity of Water = 3 parts * 6 litres/part = 18 litres.
   (Check: 42 + 18 = 60 litres).

**2. Define the change:**
   Let `x` litres of water be added.
   The quantity of milk remains unchanged.

**3. Calculate Final Quantities:**
   Final quantity of Milk = 42 litres (invariant).
   Final quantity of Water = Initial Water + Added Water = `18 + x` litres.
   Final total mixture = Initial Total + Added Water = `60 + x` litres.

**4. Use the Final Ratio to form an equation:**
   The final ratio of Milk : Water is given as 3 : 7.
   (Final Milk) / (Final Water) = 3 / 7
   `42 / (18 + x) = 3 / 7`

**5. Solve for `x`:**
   Cross-multiply:
   `42 * 7 = 3 * (18 + x)`
   `294 = 54 + 3x`
   `294 - 54 = 3x`
   `240 = 3x`
   `x = 240 / 3`
   `x = 80`

So, 80 litres of water must be added.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Invariant Quantity Focus:** The key to solving such problems quickly is focusing on the component whose quantity *does not change*. In this case, milk is invariant (42 litres).
*   **Using Final Ratio with Invariant Quantity:** Since the final ratio Milk:Water is 3:7, and we know the final Milk quantity is 42 litres, we can find the final Water quantity directly without using `x` initially.
    *   If 3 parts of milk (in the final ratio) correspond to 42 litres, then 1 part corresponds to 42 / 3 = 14 litres.
    *   The final water quantity corresponds to 7 parts in the final ratio.
    *   Final Water = 7 parts * 14 litres/part = 98 litres.
    *   The amount of water added = Final Water - Initial Water = 98 - 18 = 80 litres. This method avoids setting up and solving the algebraic equation `42 / (18 + x) = 3 / 7`.
*   **Check Units:** Ensure all quantities are in the same units (litres in this case).
