## Problem:

A container has 80 litres of a solution containing milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. If 16 litres of this solution are removed, how many litres of milk are present in the remaining solution?

(A) 30 litres
(B) 40 litres
(C) 45 litres
(D) 50 litres

## Solution:

**Method 1: Calculate Remaining Quantities**

1.  **Calculate Initial Quantities:**
    Total solution = 80 litres.
    Ratio of Milk : Water = 5 : 3.
    Sum of ratio parts = 5 + 3 = 8 parts.
    Value of 1 part = 80 litres / 8 parts = 10 litres/part.

    Initial quantity of Milk = 5 parts * 10 litres/part = 50 litres.
    Initial quantity of Water = 3 parts * 10 litres/part = 30 litres.
    (Check: 50 + 30 = 80 litres).

2.  **Calculate Amount Removed:**
    Amount of solution removed = 16 litres.
    The ratio of Milk : Water in the removed portion is the same as the original ratio, 5 : 3.
    Sum of parts = 8.

    Amount of Milk removed = (Milk ratio part / Total parts) * Amount removed
    Amount of Milk removed = (5 / 8) * 16 = 5 * 2 = 10 litres.

    Amount of Water removed = (Water ratio part / Total parts) * Amount removed
    Amount of Water removed = (3 / 8) * 16 = 3 * 2 = 6 litres.
    (Check: 10 + 6 = 16 litres).

3.  **Calculate Remaining Quantity of Milk:**
    Remaining Milk = Initial Milk - Milk Removed
    Remaining Milk = 50 - 10 = 40 litres.

**Method 2: Using Remaining Volume**

1.  **Calculate Remaining Total Volume:**
    Initial Volume = 80 litres.
    Volume Removed = 16 litres.
    Remaining Volume = 80 - 16 = 64 litres.

2.  **Ratio Remains Constant:**
    When a portion of a homogeneous mixture is removed, the ratio of the components in the remaining mixture stays the same as the original ratio.
    The ratio of Milk : Water in the remaining 64 litres is still 5 : 3.

3.  **Calculate Milk in Remaining Volume:**
    Sum of ratio parts = 5 + 3 = 8 parts.
    Quantity of Milk in remaining solution = (Milk ratio part / Total parts) * Remaining Volume
    Remaining Milk = (5 / 8) * 64
    Remaining Milk = 5 * (64 / 8)
    Remaining Milk = 5 * 8 = 40 litres.

Therefore, 40 litres of milk are present in the remaining solution.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Ratio Invariance:** The most important principle here is that removing a part of the mixture *does not* change the concentration or ratio of components in the part that is left behind.
*   **Method 2 is Faster:** Calculating the remaining total volume and then applying the original ratio to find the quantity of a component is usually quicker than calculating the amounts removed for each component separately.
*   **Focus on What's Asked:** The question asks for the milk in the *remaining* solution. Don't stop after calculating the milk removed.
