## Problem 1:

A container holds 80 litres of pure milk. From this container, 8 litres of milk were taken out and replaced with an equal amount of water. This process was repeated two more times (for a total of 3 operations). How much milk (approximately) is left in the container finally?

(A) 58.32 litres
(B) 60.00 litres
(C) 56.00 litres
(D) 59.17 litres

## Solution:

**1. Identify the parameters for the formula:**
   *   Initial Quantity of Milk (`Initial Quantity` or `Q`) = 80 litres (since it starts pure).
   *   Volume Removed in each step (`x`) = 8 litres.
   *   Total Volume of the mixture (`V`) = 80 litres (this remains constant as the removed amount is replaced).
   *   Number of operations (`n`) = 3 (initial replacement + two more times).

**2. Apply the successive replacement formula:**
   `Final Quantity = Initial Quantity * (1 - (Volume Removed / Total Volume))^n`
   `Final Milk = 80 * (1 - (8 / 80))^3`

**3. Simplify the expression:**
   `Final Milk = 80 * (1 - 1/10)^3`
   `Final Milk = 80 * (9/10)^3`
   `Final Milk = 80 * (9/10) * (9/10) * (9/10)`
   `Final Milk = 80 * (729 / 1000)`

**4. Calculate the final quantity:**
   `Final Milk = (80 * 729) / 1000`
   `Final Milk = (8 * 729) / 100`
   Calculate `8 * 729`:
   `8 * 700 = 5600`
   `8 * 20 = 160`
   `8 * 9 = 72`
   `Total = 5600 + 160 + 72 = 5832`

   `Final Milk = 5832 / 100`
   `Final Milk = 58.32` litres.

Therefore, approximately 58.32 litres of milk are left in the container after 3 operations.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Formula Application:** Recognize this as a standard successive replacement problem and directly apply the formula `Final = Initial * (1 - x/V)^n`.
*   **Fraction Simplification:** Simplify the fraction `x/V` (8/80 to 1/10) before proceeding with the calculation `(1 - x/V)`. This makes cubing easier.
*   **Calculation Strategy:** Calculate `(1 - x/V)^n` first (here, `(9/10)^3 = 729/1000`) and then multiply by the initial quantity. Avoid large intermediate numbers if possible.
*   **Approximation Check:** After one operation, milk left = `80 * (9/10) = 72`. After two, `72 * (9/10) = 64.8`. After three, `64.8 * (9/10) = 58.32`. This step-by-step approach can be used for checking or if `n` is small, but the formula is faster for larger `n`. The result must be less than the quantity after the previous step.
*   **Units:** Ensure all quantities are in the same unit (litres). The final answer will be in litres.

## Problem 2:

A can contains a mixture of two liquids, A and B, in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 litres of the mixture are drawn off and the can is filled with the same amount of liquid B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7 : 9. How many litres of liquid A were contained in the can initially?

(A) 10 litres
(B) 20 litres
(C) 21 litres
(D) 25 litres

## Solution:

**Method 1: Step-by-Step Tracking**

1.  **Initial State:**
    Let the initial quantities of A and B be `7x` litres and `5x` litres respectively.
    Initial Total mixture = `7x + 5x = 12x` litres.

2.  **Mixture Drawn Off:**
    9 litres of the mixture are drawn off. The liquids A and B are removed in the same ratio (7:5).
    Amount of A removed = (7 / (7+5)) * 9 = (7/12) * 9 = 21/4 = 5.25 litres.
    Amount of B removed = (5 / (7+5)) * 9 = (5/12) * 9 = 15/4 = 3.75 litres.

3.  **Quantities Remaining After Removal:**
    Remaining A = `7x - 5.25` litres.
    Remaining B = `5x - 3.75` litres.
    Remaining Total = `12x - 9` litres.

4.  **Replacement:**
    The can is filled with 9 litres of liquid B.
    New quantity of A = `7x - 5.25` litres (unchanged).
    New quantity of B = (Remaining B) + 9 = `(5x - 3.75) + 9 = 5x + 5.25` litres.

5.  **Final Ratio:**
    The new ratio A : B is given as 7 : 9.
    (New A) / (New B) = 7 / 9
    `(7x - 5.25) / (5x + 5.25) = 7 / 9`

6.  **Solve for `x`:**
    `9 * (7x - 5.25) = 7 * (5x + 5.25)`
    `63x - 47.25 = 35x + 36.75`
    `63x - 35x = 36.75 + 47.25`
    `28x = 84`
    `x = 84 / 28`
    `x = 3`

7.  **Calculate Initial Quantity of A:**
    Initial quantity of A = `7x = 7 * 3 = 21` litres.

**Method 2: Focusing on the Invariant Component during Replacement**

1.  **Initial Ratio:** A : B = 7 : 5. Total = 12 parts.
2.  **After Removal (but before replacement):** When 9 litres are removed, the *ratio* of A and B in the remaining mixture is still 7 : 5. Let the remaining quantity be `V - 9` litres, where V is the initial volume `12x`.
3.  **Replacement:** 9 litres of B are added. Only B is added. A's quantity doesn't change *during this specific step*.
4.  **Final Ratio:** A : B = 7 : 9.
5.  **Logic:** Consider the quantity of A in the mixture. Its proportion in the final mixture is 7 / (7+9) = 7/16. This final amount of A is the same amount that was present *after* removal but *before* replacement.
    The quantity of A *after removal* = `(7/12) * (Initial Total - 9)`
    The quantity of A *in the final mixture* = `(7/16) * (Final Total)`
    Since 9L was removed and 9L was added, the Final Total volume is the same as the Initial Total volume (`12x`).
    So, quantity of A after removal = quantity of A in final mixture.
    `(7/12) * (12x - 9) = (7/16) * (12x)`
6.  **Solve for `x`:**
    Divide both sides by 7:
    `(1/12) * (12x - 9) = (1/16) * (12x)`
    Multiply both sides by 48 (LCM of 12 and 16):
    `4 * (12x - 9) = 3 * (12x)`
    `48x - 36 = 36x`
    `48x - 36x = 36`
    `12x = 36`
    `x = 3`
7.  **Calculate Initial Quantity of A:**
    Initial quantity of A = `7x = 7 * 3 = 21` litres.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Invariant Ratio on Removal:** Remember that removing a portion of the mixture does *not* change the ratio of components in the remaining part.
*   **Focus on Unchanged Component:** Method 2 is generally faster. Identify the component whose quantity is *not* directly changed during the *replacement step* (here, liquid A). Express its quantity before and after the replacement step and equate them, considering the change in total volume and proportions.
*   **Total Volume:** Notice that when the amount removed is equal to the amount replaced, the final total volume is the same as the initial total volume. This simplifies calculations in Method 2.
*   **Fractions vs Decimals:** While Method 1 used decimals, using fractions (`21/4`, `15/4`) might be less error-prone during calculations. `(7x - 21/4) / (5x - 15/4 + 9) = 7/9`. Simplify the denominator: `5x + (36-15)/4 = 5x + 21/4`. So, `(7x - 21/4) / (5x + 21/4) = 7/9`. Multiply numerator/denominator by 4: `(28x - 21) / (20x + 21) = 7/9`. `9(28x - 21) = 7(20x + 21)`. `252x - 189 = 140x + 147`. `112x = 336`. `x = 3`. Initial A = `7x = 21`.
