# Example: Finding SP when CP and Profit % given

Tags: #quant #profit_and_loss #selling_price #cost_price #profit_percentage #example #overhead_costs #unit_price

Parent Concept: [[03 - Profit and Loss/02 - Finding SP CP given Profit Loss %]]

## Problem

A shopkeeper purchased 40 identical toys for ₹180 each. He spent a total of ₹480 on packaging for all the toys together. He wants to achieve an overall profit of 20% on his total investment. What should be the selling price of *each* toy?

## Analysis

This problem requires several steps:
1.  Calculate the total purchase cost of all toys.
2.  Calculate the total effective Cost Price (CP) by adding the packaging cost (overhead).
3.  Calculate the total Selling Price (SP) required to achieve a 20% profit on the total effective CP.
4.  Calculate the selling price per toy by dividing the total SP by the number of toys.

## Solution

1.  **Calculate Total Purchase Cost:**
    *   Number of toys = 40
    *   Cost per toy = ₹180
    *   Total Purchase Cost = 40 * ₹180 = ₹7200

2.  **Calculate Total Effective Cost Price (CP):**
    *   Total Purchase Cost = ₹7200
    *   Packaging Cost = ₹480
    *   Total Effective CP = Total Purchase Cost + Packaging Cost
    *   Total Effective CP = ₹7200 + ₹480 = ₹7680
    *   This is the total investment on which the 20% profit is desired.

3.  **Calculate Total Selling Price (SP) for 20% Profit:**
    *   Total Effective CP = ₹7680
    *   Desired Profit % = 20%
    *   Using the formula: `SP = CP * [(100 + Profit %) / 100]`
    *   Total SP = ₹7680 * [(100 + 20) / 100]
    *   Total SP = ₹7680 * [120 / 100]
    *   Total SP = ₹7680 * (12 / 10)
    *   Total SP = ₹7680 * 1.2

    *Calculation:*
    *   `7680 * 1.2 = 7680 * (1 + 0.2) = 7680 + (7680 * 0.2)`
    *   `10% of 7680 = 768`
    *   `20% of 7680 = 768 * 2 = 1536`
    *   `Total SP = 7680 + 1536 = 9216`

    *Alternatively using multiplication factor:*
    *   Factor = `1 + Profit % / 100 = 1 + 20 / 100 = 1.2`
    *   Total SP = Total Effective CP * Factor
    *   Total SP = ₹7680 * 1.2 = ₹9216

4.  **Calculate Selling Price per Toy:**
    *   Total SP = ₹9216
    *   Number of toys = 40
    *   SP per toy = Total SP / Number of toys
    *   SP per toy = ₹9216 / 40
    *   SP per toy = ₹921.6 / 4
    *   SP per toy = ₹230.4

## Shortcut/Tip

*   First find the *total effective CP*.
*   Calculate the *total SP* based on the overall profit required.
*   Only divide by the number of items at the *very end* to find the price per item. Avoid calculating profit per item individually if overheads are given for the whole lot.
*   Multiplying by 1.2 (for 20% profit) is equivalent to adding 20% (or 1/5th) of the value to itself. `7680 + (7680/5) = 7680 + 1536 = 9216`.

**Answer:** The selling price of each toy should be ₹230.40.
