# Example: Calculating SP after Two Successive Discounts

Tags: #quant #profit_and_loss #discount #successive_discounts #marked_price #selling_price #example

Parent Concept: [[03 - Profit and Loss/04 - Successive Discounts]]

## Problem

A television set is listed with a Marked Price (MP) of ₹25,000. The showroom offers two successive discounts: first 15% and then 8% on the reduced price. What is the final Selling Price (SP) of the television set?

## Analysis

This problem involves applying two discounts sequentially to the Marked Price. The second discount (8%) is applied to the price *after* the first discount (15%) has been deducted. We need to calculate the final price the customer pays.

## Solution Method 1: Step-by-Step Calculation

1.  **Calculate the price after the first discount (15%):**
    *   Marked Price (MP) = ₹25,000
    *   First Discount Amount = 15% of ₹25,000
    *   First Discount Amount = (15 / 100) * 25000 = 15 * 250 = ₹3750
    *   Price after 1st Discount = MP - First Discount Amount
    *   Price after 1st Discount = ₹25,000 - ₹3750 = ₹21,250

2.  **Calculate the price after the second discount (8% on the reduced price):**
    *   Price before 2nd discount = ₹21,250
    *   Second Discount Amount = 8% of ₹21,250
    *   Second Discount Amount = (8 / 100) * 21250 = (8 * 21250) / 100
    *   `8 * 21250 = 170000`
    *   Second Discount Amount = 170000 / 100 = ₹1700
    *   Final Selling Price (SP) = Price after 1st Discount - Second Discount Amount
    *   Final Selling Price (SP) = ₹21,250 - ₹1700 = ₹19,550

## Solution Method 2: Using Multiplication Factors

1.  **Identify the discount percentages:**
    *   D1 = 15%
    *   D2 = 8%

2.  **Calculate the corresponding factors:**
    *   Factor 1 (for 15% discount) = `1 - D1/100 = 1 - 15/100 = 0.85`
    *   Factor 2 (for 8% discount) = `1 - D2/100 = 1 - 8/100 = 0.92`

3.  **Apply the formula SP = MP * Factor1 * Factor2:**
    *   SP = ₹25,000 * 0.85 * 0.92

4.  **Calculate the result:**
    *   `SP = 25000 * (0.85 * 0.92)`
    *   Calculate `0.85 * 0.92`:
        *   `85 * 92 = 85 * (90 + 2) = (85 * 90) + (85 * 2) = 7650 + 170 = 7820`
        *   Since 0.85 has 2 decimal places and 0.92 has 2 decimal places, the result has 4 decimal places: `0.7820`
    *   `SP = 25000 * 0.7820`
    *   `SP = 25 * 1000 * 0.782 = 25 * 782`
    *   `25 * 782 = 25 * (800 - 18) = (25 * 800) - (25 * 18) = 20000 - 450 = 19550`
    *   SP = ₹19,550

## Shortcut/Tip

*   The multiplication factor method (`SP = MP * F1 * F2`) is often quicker and less prone to intermediate calculation errors than the step-by-step method, especially with cleaner factors or a calculator.
*   Remember that the second discount applies to the *reduced* price.

**Answer:** The final Selling Price (SP) of the television set is ₹19,550.
