# Example: Finding Net Selling Price after Discount

Tags: #quant #profit_and_loss #markup #discount #marked_price #selling_price #cost_price #profit_percentage #example #sequential_calculation

Parent Concept: [[03 - Profit and Loss/03 - Markup and Discount]]

## Problem

A retailer buys an item for ₹1500 (Cost Price). He marks it up by 40%. During a sale, he offers a 15% discount on this marked price.
(a) What is the final Selling Price (SP) of the item?
(b) What is the overall profit or loss percentage made by the retailer?

## Analysis

This problem requires calculating the final SP after applying a markup and then a discount. It also asks for the overall profit/loss percentage, which requires comparing the final SP back to the original CP.
Steps:
1.  Calculate the Marked Price (MP) by applying the markup percentage to the CP.
2.  Calculate the final Selling Price (SP) by applying the discount percentage to the MP. This answers part (a).
3.  Calculate the overall profit or loss amount (SP - CP).
4.  Calculate the overall profit or loss percentage based on the CP. This answers part (b).

## Solution

**Part (a): Find the Selling Price (SP)**

1.  **Calculate the Marked Price (MP):**
    *   Cost Price (CP) = ₹1500
    *   Markup % = 40%
    *   MP = CP * (1 + Markup % / 100)
    *   MP = 1500 * (1 + 40 / 100)
    *   MP = 1500 * (1 + 0.40)
    *   MP = 1500 * 1.40
    *   `1500 * 1.4 = 150 * 14 = 150 * (10 + 4) = 1500 + 600 = 2100`
    *   MP = ₹2100

2.  **Calculate the Selling Price (SP) after Discount:**
    *   Marked Price (MP) = ₹2100
    *   Discount % = 15%
    *   SP = MP * (1 - Discount % / 100)
    *   SP = 2100 * (1 - 15 / 100)
    *   SP = 2100 * (1 - 0.15)
    *   SP = 2100 * 0.85

    *Calculation:*
    *   `2100 * 0.85 = 2100 * (1 - 0.15) = 2100 - (2100 * 0.15)`
    *   `10% of 2100 = 210`
    *   `5% of 2100 = 105`
    *   `15% of 2100 = 210 + 105 = 315`
    *   `SP = 2100 - 315 = 1785`
    *   SP = ₹1785

**Part (b): Find the Overall Profit or Loss Percentage**

1.  **Compare Final SP with Original CP:**
    *   Final Selling Price (SP) = ₹1785
    *   Original Cost Price (CP) = ₹1500
    *   Since SP > CP, there is an overall profit.

2.  **Calculate the Profit Amount:**
    *   Profit = SP - CP
    *   Profit = ₹1785 - ₹1500 = ₹285

3.  **Calculate the Profit Percentage:**
    *   Profit % is calculated on the CP.
    *   Profit % = (Profit / CP) * 100 %
    *   Profit % = (285 / 1500) * 100 %
    *   Simplify: `Profit % = (285 / 15) %`
    *   `285 / 15 = (150 + 135) / 15 = 10 + 9 = 19`
    *   Profit % = 19 %

## Alternative (Using Net Factor for Part b)

Calculate the net effect of a 40% markup (+40%) followed by a 15% discount (-15%) using the successive percentage change formula:
`Net % Change = a + b + (ab/100)`
`Net % Change = (+40) + (-15) + ((+40) * (-15) / 100)`
`Net % Change = 40 - 15 + (-600 / 100)`
`Net % Change = 25 - 6`
`Net % Change = +19 %`
This directly gives the overall profit percentage.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   Calculate MP first, then apply the discount to find SP.
*   Overall Profit/Loss% compares the final SP to the initial CP.
*   The net effect of markup and discount can be found using successive percentage change logic, which directly yields the overall profit/loss percentage.

**Answer:**
(a) The final Selling Price (SP) is ₹1785.
(b) The overall profit percentage made by the retailer is 19%.
