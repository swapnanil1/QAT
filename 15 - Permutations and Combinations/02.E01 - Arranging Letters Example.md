# Arranging Letters Example

**Problem:**
In how many different ways can the letters of the word 'FORMULA' be arranged such that the vowels always come together?

(A) 120
(B) 720
(C) 5040
(D) 144

## Solution:

This problem involves arranging the letters of a word with a specific constraint (vowels together). We need to use the concepts of permutations and the multiplication principle, as explained in [[15 - Permutations and Combinations/02 - Permutation (Arrangement) Formula (nPr).md|the permutation concept note]].

**Step 1: Identify Letters, Vowels, and Consonants**
The word is 'FORMULA'.
Total letters (n) = 7 (all distinct).
Vowels = O, U, A (3 vowels).
Consonants = F, R, M, L (4 consonants).

**Step 2: Apply the 'Grouping' Technique for the Constraint**
Since the vowels (O, U, A) must always be together, treat this group of vowels as a single unit or 'block'.

**Step 3: Determine the Number of Units to Arrange**
Now consider the units to be arranged:
Unit 1: (OUA) - the vowel group
Unit 2: F
Unit 3: R
Unit 4: M
Unit 5: L
We have 5 distinct units to arrange.

**Step 4: Calculate the Number of Ways to Arrange the Units**
These 5 distinct units can be arranged among themselves in `5!` ways.
Arrangement of units = `5! = 5 * 4 * 3 * 2 * 1 = 120` ways.

**Step 5: Calculate the Number of Ways to Arrange Letters Within the Group**
The letters within the vowel group (OUA) can also be arranged among themselves. Since there are 3 distinct vowels, they can be arranged in `3!` ways.
Arrangement within vowel group = `3! = 3 * 2 * 1 = 6` ways. (OUA, OAU, UOA, UAO, AOU, AUO)

**Step 6: Apply the Multiplication Principle**
To get the total number of arrangements satisfying the condition, multiply the number of ways to arrange the units by the number of ways to arrange the letters within the vowel group.
Total Arrangements = (Arrangements of units) * (Arrangements within the vowel group)
Total Arrangements = `5! * 3!`
Total Arrangements = 120 * 6
Total Arrangements = 720

**Step 7: State the Final Answer**
The letters of the word 'FORMULA' can be arranged in 720 ways such that the vowels always come together. This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **'Together' Constraint = Grouping:** Whenever a problem requires certain items to always be together, treat them as a single unit.
2.  **Two-Step Multiplication:** Calculate permutations in two steps: (1) Arrange the units (including the grouped unit). (2) Arrange the items *within* the grouped unit. Multiply the results.
3.  **Identify 'n' for Each Step:** In Step 4, 'n' was 5 (for the 5 units). In Step 5, 'n' was 3 (for the 3 vowels). Use the correct 'n' for each factorial calculation.
4.  **Distinct vs. Non-Distinct:** This problem had all distinct letters. If the word had repeated letters (e.g., 'MATHEMATICS'), you would need to use the permutation formula with repetitions for arranging the units and potentially within the group if vowels were repeated.
5.  **Common Distractors:** Option (A) 120 is just `5!` (arranging the units but forgetting internal vowel arrangement). Option (C) 5040 is `7!` (total arrangements without any constraint). Option (D) 144 might arise from confusing the counts (`4! * 3!`).
6.  **Contrast: 'Never Together':** If the question asked for vowels *never* together, the strategy would be: (Total Arrangements without constraint) - (Arrangements where vowels ARE together) = `7! - 720 = 5040 - 720 = 4320`.
