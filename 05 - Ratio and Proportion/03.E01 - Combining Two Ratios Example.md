## Problem:

A sum of money is to be distributed among P, Q, and R. The ratio of the shares of P and Q is 3 : 5. The ratio of the shares of Q and R is 2 : 7. If the total sum is ₹ 5100, what is the difference between the shares of R and P?

(A) ₹ 2100
(B) ₹ 2900
(C) ₹ 1500
(D) ₹ 2400

## Solution:

**1. State the given ratios:**
   `P : Q = 3 : 5`
   `Q : R = 2 : 7`

**2. Identify the common term:**
   The common term linking the two ratios is Q.

**3. Make the value corresponding to the common term (Q) equal in both ratios:**
   The values for Q are 5 (in P:Q) and 2 (in Q:R).
   Find the LCM of 5 and 2, which is 10.
   *   Multiply the first ratio (`P : Q = 3 : 5`) by `10 / 5 = 2`.
       `P : Q = (3 * 2) : (5 * 2) = 6 : 10`
   *   Multiply the second ratio (`Q : R = 2 : 7`) by `10 / 2 = 5`.
       `Q : R = (2 * 5) : (7 * 5) = 10 : 35`

**4. Write the combined ratio P : Q : R:**
   Since the value for Q is now 10 in both adjusted ratios, we can combine them:
   `P : Q : R = 6 : 10 : 35`

**5. Calculate the sum of the ratio parts:**
   Total parts = 6 + 10 + 35 = 51 parts.

**6. Determine the value of one ratio part:**
   The total sum distributed is ₹ 5100.
   Value of 1 part = Total Sum / Total Parts
   Value of 1 part = 5100 / 51 = ₹ 100.

**7. Calculate the required difference (R's share - P's share):**
   *Method 1: Calculate individual shares first.*
     P's share = 6 parts = 6 * 100 = ₹ 600
     R's share = 35 parts = 35 * 100 = ₹ 3500
     Difference = R's share - P's share = 3500 - 600 = ₹ 2900.

   *Method 2: Calculate the difference in ratio parts first.*
     Difference in parts = R's parts - P's parts = 35 - 6 = 29 parts.
     Difference amount = Difference in parts * Value of 1 part
     Difference amount = 29 * 100 = ₹ 2900.

Therefore, the difference between the shares of R and P is ₹ 2900.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Difference:** If only the difference (or sum) between specific shares is asked, calculate the difference (or sum) of their corresponding ratio parts *first*. Then multiply this result by the value of one part. This avoids calculating unnecessary individual shares (Method 2 above is faster).
*   **LCM Method:** Using the LCM to equalize the common term is reliable. Alternatively, multiply the first ratio by the common term's value from the second ratio, and multiply the second ratio by the common term's value from the first ratio (i.e., multiply `3:5` by 2, and `2:7` by 5). This achieves the same result: `P:Q = 6:10`, `Q:R = 10:35`.
*   **Verification:** Quickly check if the total shares calculated match the total sum: 600 (P) + 1000 (Q) + 3500 (R) = 5100. This confirms the ratio combination and value per part are correct.
*   **Read Carefully:** Ensure you answer what is asked (difference R-P, not P-R or Q-P, etc.).
