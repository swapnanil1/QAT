## Problem:

A train covers 120 km in 2 hours. A bus covers 1500 meters in 1 minute. Find the ratio of their average speeds (Train : Bus).

(A) 3 : 2
(B) 2 : 3
(C) 12 : 5
(D) 5 : 12

## Solution:

**1. Calculate the speed of the train:**
   Speed = Distance / Time
   Speed of Train = 120 km / 2 hours = 60 km/hr.

**2. Calculate the speed of the bus:**
   Speed = Distance / Time
   Distance = 1500 meters
   Time = 1 minute = 60 seconds
   Speed of Bus = 1500 m / 60 sec = 150 / 6 m/sec = 25 m/sec.

**3. Convert speeds to the same unit (m/sec):**
   We know that 1 km/hr = (1000 m) / (3600 sec) = 5/18 m/sec.
   Speed of Train in m/sec = 60 * (5/18) m/sec
   Speed of Train = (10 * 5) / 3 m/sec = 50/3 m/sec.
   Speed of Bus = 25 m/sec.

**4. Form the ratio of the speeds (Train : Bus):**
   Ratio = (Speed of Train) : (Speed of Bus)
   Ratio = (50/3) : 25

**5. Simplify the ratio:**
   To remove the fraction, multiply both sides by 3:
   Ratio = (50/3 * 3) : (25 * 3)
   Ratio = 50 : 75
   Now, divide both sides by the greatest common divisor (GCD), which is 25:
   Ratio = (50 / 25) : (75 / 25)
   Ratio = 2 : 3

Therefore, the ratio of the speed of the train to the speed of the bus is 2 : 3.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Unit Consistency:** The most crucial step in ratio problems involving physical quantities (like speed, length, weight, time) is to ensure both quantities are expressed in the same units before forming the ratio. Converting to base SI units (like meters/second for speed) is often reliable.
*   **Conversion Factor:** Remember the key conversion: `1 km/hr = 5/18 m/sec`. Using this directly saves calculation steps.
*   **Simplification:** Always simplify the final ratio to its lowest terms by dividing both antecedent and consequent by their GCD.
*   **Order Matters:** Pay close attention to the order requested in the ratio (Train : Bus vs. Bus : Train). Option (A) is the inverse ratio, a common distractor. Option (C) arises if one compares 60 km/hr directly with 25 m/sec without conversion.
