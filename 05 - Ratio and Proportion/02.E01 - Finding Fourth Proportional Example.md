## Problem:

Find the fourth proportional to 0.12, 0.21, and 8.

(A) 1.4
(B) 14
(C) 1.8
(D) 18

## Solution:

Let the four numbers in proportion be `a, b, c, x`.
We are given `a = 0.12`, `b = 0.21`, `c = 8`. We need to find `x`.

The proportion is `a : b :: c : x`, which means `a/b = c/x`.

Using the property of proportions (product of extremes = product of means):
`a * x = b * c`

Substitute the given values:
`0.12 * x = 0.21 * 8`

Now, solve for `x`:
`x = (0.21 * 8) / 0.12`

To simplify the calculation, remove the decimals by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 100 (or notice that both 0.21 and 0.12 have two decimal places):
`x = (21 * 8) / 12`

Now, simplify the fraction:
*   Divide 12 and 8 by their GCD, 4: `12 / 4 = 3`, `8 / 4 = 2`.
    `x = (21 * 2) / 3`
*   Divide 21 by 3: `21 / 3 = 7`.
    `x = 7 * 2`
    `x = 14`

Therefore, the fourth proportional is 14.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Formula:** Remember the formula for the fourth proportional `x` to `a, b, c`: `x = (b * c) / a`. Apply it directly.
*   **Decimal Handling:** When dealing with decimals, try to eliminate them early by multiplying both terms involving decimals by the same power of 10 (e.g., `0.21 / 0.12` is the same as `21 / 12`). This converts the problem into integer calculations, reducing potential errors.
*   **Simplification:** Simplify the fraction `(b * c) / a` *before* performing the final multiplication. Look for common factors between `a` and `b` or `a` and `c`. In this case, simplifying `21/12` to `7/4` first gives `x = (7/4) * 8 = 7 * (8/4) = 7 * 2 = 14`.
*   **Estimation:** Check if the answer makes sense. `0.12` is a bit more than half of `0.21`. So, `x` should be a bit less than double `8`. `14` fits this estimation. Options like 1.4 or 1.8 are too small.
