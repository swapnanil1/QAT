# Two Trains Crossing Each Other Example

**Problem:**
Two trains, one 100 meters long and the other 80 meters long, are running on parallel tracks. The first train runs at a speed of 36 km/hr and the second train runs at a speed of 54 km/hr. If they are running towards each other (opposite directions), how long will it take them to completely cross each other from the moment they meet?

(A) 6.0 seconds
(B) 7.2 seconds
(C) 18.0 seconds
(D) 2.0 seconds

## Solution:
This problem involves two trains moving in opposite directions and requires the concept of [[11 - Time, Speed and Distance/03 - Relative Speed (Same Opposite Direction).md|Relative Speed]].

**Step 1: Identify Given Information**
Length of the first train (`L1`) = 100 meters.
Speed of the first train (`S1`) = 36 km/hr.
Length of the second train (`L2`) = 80 meters.
Speed of the second train (`S2`) = 54 km/hr.
Direction: Opposite.

**Step 2: Determine the Total Distance to be Covered**
When two trains cross each other, the total distance to be covered for them to completely pass one another is the sum of their lengths.
Total Distance (`D`) = `L1 + L2`
`D` = 100 m + 80 m = 180 meters.

**Step 3: Ensure Consistent Units for Speed**
The distances are in meters, so speeds must be converted from km/hr to m/s for time to be in seconds.
Conversion factor: `1 km/hr = 5/18 m/s`.

Speed of the first train (`S1`) in m/s:
`S1` = 36 * (5/18) m/s
`S1` = (36/18) * 5 m/s
`S1` = 2 * 5 = 10 m/s.

Speed of the second train (`S2`) in m/s:
`S2` = 54 * (5/18) m/s
`S2` = (54/18) * 5 m/s
`S2` = 3 * 5 = 15 m/s.

**Step 4: Calculate Relative Speed**
Since the trains are moving in opposite directions, their relative speed (`Sr`) is the sum of their individual speeds.
`Sr = S1 + S2`
`Sr` = 10 m/s + 15 m/s = 25 m/s.

**Step 5: Calculate the Time Taken to Cross**
Use the formula `Time = Distance / Speed`. Here, Distance is the Total Distance (`D`) and Speed is the Relative Speed (`Sr`).
Time (`T`) = `D / Sr`
`T` = 180 meters / 25 m/s
`T` = 180 / 25 seconds

Simplify the fraction: Divide numerator and denominator by 5.
`T` = (180/5) / (25/5) seconds
`T` = 36 / 5 seconds
`T` = 7.2 seconds.

It will take 7.2 seconds for the trains to completely cross each other.

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Recognize Scenario:** Two trains crossing. Total Distance = `L1 + L2`.
2.  **Check Direction:** Opposite directions mean Relative Speed = `S1 + S2`. (If same direction, Relative Speed = `|S1 - S2|`).
3.  **Unit Consistency is Key:** Convert speeds (km/hr to m/s using `* 5/18`) *before* calculating relative speed or time. Errors often occur here.
4.  **Combine:** Calculate `T = (L1 + L2) / (S1 + S2)` after ensuring all units are consistent (meters and m/s). In this case: `T = (100 + 80) / (10 + 15) = 180 / 25 = 7.2` seconds.
