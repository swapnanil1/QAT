# Objects Moving in Opposite Direction Example

**Problem:**

Two towns, A and B, are 225 km apart. A cyclist starts from town A at 10:00 AM and cycles towards town B at a speed of 20 km/hr. At the same time, another cyclist starts from town B and cycles towards town A at a speed of 25 km/hr. At what time will the two cyclists meet?

(A) 2:00 PM
(B) 2:30 PM
(C) 3:00 PM
(D) 3:30 PM

## Solution:

This problem involves two objects (cyclists) moving towards each other from a fixed distance apart. We will use the concept of relative speed for objects moving in opposite directions, as explained in [[11 - Time, Speed and Distance/03 - Relative Speed (Same Opposite Direction).md]].

**Step 1: Identify the Given Information**
Distance between towns A and B = 225 km.
Speed of cyclist from A (S_A) = 20 km/hr.
Speed of cyclist from B (S_B) = 25 km/hr.
Both cyclists start at the same time (10:00 AM).

**Step 2: Calculate the Relative Speed**
Since the cyclists are moving towards each other (opposite directions), their relative speed is the sum of their individual speeds. This represents the rate at which the distance between them is closing.
Relative Speed (`S_rel`) = `S_A + S_B`
`S_rel` = 20 km/hr + 25 km/hr = 45 km/hr.

**Step 3: Calculate the Time to Meet**
The time taken for the cyclists to meet is the initial distance between them divided by their relative speed.
Time = Distance / Relative Speed
Time = 225 km / 45 km/hr

Calculation: 225 / 45
(Alternatively: 225 / 5 = 45, so 225 / 45 = 5)
Time = 5 hours.

**Step 4: Determine the Meeting Time**
The cyclists start at 10:00 AM and will meet after 5 hours.
Meeting Time = Starting Time + Time Taken
Meeting Time = 10:00 AM + 5 hours = 15:00 hours.

Converting 15:00 hours to the 12-hour clock format gives 3:00 PM.

The two cyclists will meet at 3:00 PM. This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

1.  **Check Start Times:** Verify both start simultaneously (they do).
2.  **Direction -> Formula:** They move towards each other (opposite directions). Use `S_rel = S1 + S2`.
3.  **Calculate Time:** `Time = Distance / S_rel = 225 / (20+25) = 225 / 45 = 5` hours.
4.  **Calculate Final Time:** Add the duration to the start time: `10:00 AM + 5 hours = 3:00 PM`.

Focus on identifying the key elements: initial distance, individual speeds, direction of travel, and start times. Apply the correct relative speed formula based on the direction.
