# Basic Formula (D=S*T) and Unit Conversions

**Introduction:**

Time, Speed, and Distance (TSD) problems are a fundamental part of quantitative aptitude. They involve the relationship between the speed at which an object travels, the time it takes, and the distance it covers.

**Core Formula:**

The relationship between Distance (D), Speed (S), and Time (T) is defined by the formula:

`Distance = Speed × Time`
`D = S * T`

This formula can be rearranged to find Speed or Time:

*   `Speed = Distance / Time` (`S = D / T`)
*   `Time = Distance / Speed` (`T = D / S`)

**Units:**

Consistency of units is **crucial** in TSD problems. Ensure all quantities are expressed in a compatible system before applying the formula.

*   **Distance:** Commonly measured in kilometers (km), meters (m).
*   **Time:** Commonly measured in hours (hr), minutes (min), seconds (sec).
*   **Speed:** Derived from distance and time units. Common units are:
    *   Kilometers per hour (km/hr or kmph)
    *   Meters per second (m/s or mps)

**Unit Conversions (VVIP):**

Being able to quickly convert between common speed units is essential.

1.  **km/hr to m/s:**
    *   1 km = 1000 m
    *   1 hr = 3600 sec
    *   So, 1 km/hr = `1000 m / 3600 sec` = `10 / 36 m/s` = `5 / 18 m/s`.
    *   **Rule:** To convert km/hr to m/s, multiply by **5/18**.
    *   `Speed (m/s) = Speed (km/hr) * (5/18)`

2.  **m/s to km/hr:**
    *   Using the reverse of the above:
    *   1 m/s = `(18/5) km/hr`.
    *   **Rule:** To convert m/s to km/hr, multiply by **18/5**.
    *   `Speed (km/hr) = Speed (m/s) * (18/5)`

**Memorization Tip:**
*   km/hr (bigger unit) to m/s (smaller unit) -> Multiply by the smaller fraction (5/18).
*   m/s (smaller unit) to km/hr (bigger unit) -> Multiply by the larger fraction (18/5).

**Other Conversions:**

*   1 hour = 60 minutes
*   1 minute = 60 seconds
*   1 km = 1000 meters

Always convert time units (minutes to hours, seconds to hours/minutes) or distance units (meters to km) as needed to match the speed unit before using the D=S*T formula.

**Example:** How far does a car travel in 20 minutes at a speed of 60 km/hr?
1.  Speed = 60 km/hr.
2.  Time = 20 minutes. Convert to hours: Time = 20 / 60 hr = 1/3 hr.
3.  Distance = Speed * Time = 60 km/hr * (1/3) hr = 20 km.
