# Train Crossing Platform Bridge Example

**Problem:**
A train passes a man standing on a 240-meter long platform in 8 seconds and passes the entire platform in 20 seconds. What is the length of the train?

(A) 120 m
(B) 160 m
(C) 200 m
(D) 180 m

## Solution:

This problem involves two scenarios with the same train (same length and speed) but different crossing situations. We can set up equations based on the [[11 - Time, Speed and Distance/01 - Basic Formula (D=S T) and Unit Conversions.md|Distance = Speed × Time]] formula.

**Step 1: Define Variables and Identify Given Information**
Let the length of the train be `Lt` meters.
Let the speed of the train be `St` m/s.
Length of the platform (`Lp`) = 240 meters.
Time to cross the man (`T1`) = 8 seconds.
Time to cross the platform (`T2`) = 20 seconds.

**Step 2: Formulate Equations based on Scenarios**

*   **Scenario 1: Crossing the Man**
    Distance covered = Length of the train (`Lt`).
    Time taken = `T1` = 8 s.
    Equation 1: `Lt = St * T1` => `Lt = St * 8`

*   **Scenario 2: Crossing the Platform**
    Distance covered = Length of the train + Length of the platform (`Lt + Lp`).
    Time taken = `T2` = 20 s.
    Equation 2: `Lt + Lp = St * T2` => `Lt + 240 = St * 20`

**Step 3: Solve the System of Equations**
We have two equations:
1.  `Lt = 8 * St`
2.  `Lt + 240 = 20 * St`

From Equation 1, we can express `St` in terms of `Lt`:
`St = Lt / 8`

Substitute this expression for `St` into Equation 2:
`Lt + 240 = 20 * (Lt / 8)`
`Lt + 240 = (20 * Lt) / 8`
Simplify the fraction `20/8` by dividing both by 4:
`Lt + 240 = (5 * Lt) / 2`

To eliminate the fraction, multiply both sides by 2:
2 * (`Lt + 240`) = `5 * Lt`
`2 * Lt + 480 = 5 * Lt`

Rearrange the terms to solve for `Lt`:
`480 = 5 * Lt - 2 * Lt`
`480 = 3 * Lt`

**Step 4: Calculate the Length of the Train**
Divide both sides by 3:
`Lt` = 480 / 3
`Lt` = 160

The length of the train is 160 meters.

*(Self-check: If Lt = 160 m, then St = 160 / 8 = 20 m/s. Crossing platform distance = 160 + 240 = 400 m. Time = 400 / 20 = 20 s. Matches given info.)*

## Shortcut/Tip
The core insight is that the *difference* in time (20 s - 8 s = 12 s) is the time taken for the train, moving at its speed `St`, to cover the *difference* in distance (which is `(Lt + Lp) - Lt = Lp`).
So, the train covers the platform's length (240 m) in 12 seconds.

1.  Calculate time difference: `ΔT = T2 - T1` = 20 - 8 = 12 s.
2.  This `ΔT` is the time to cover the platform length `Lp`.
3.  Calculate Speed: `St = Lp / ΔT` = 240 m / 12 s = 20 m/s.
4.  Calculate Train Length: Use the time to cross the man (`T1`) and the calculated speed (`St`).
    `Lt = St * T1` = 20 * 8 = 160 m.

This method avoids solving simultaneous equations directly and is significantly faster.
