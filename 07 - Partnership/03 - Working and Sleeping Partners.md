# Working and Sleeping Partners

#quant #partnership #compound_partnership #profit_sharing #working_partner #sleeping_partner #salary #commission

## Definitions

In a partnership, partners can have different levels of involvement beyond just contributing capital. This leads to distinctions like:

1.  **Working Partner (Active Partner):**
    *   Contributes capital to the business.
    *   Actively participates in the day-to-day operations and management of the business.
    *   May receive remuneration for their work *in addition* to their share of profit based on investment. This remuneration can be in the form of a salary, commission, or a fixed percentage of the total profit, as agreed upon in the partnership deed.

2.  **Sleeping Partner (Silent Partner / Dormant Partner):**
    *   Contributes capital to the business.
    *   Does *not* actively participate in the management or operations.
    *   Their share of profit or loss is typically determined solely by their investment ratio, according to the partnership agreement. They usually do not receive salaries or commissions for work.

## Profit Distribution with Working Partners

When a partnership includes working partners who are entitled to remuneration (salary, commission, etc.), the profit distribution follows these steps:

1.  **Calculate Total Profit:** Determine the gross profit earned by the business over the specified period.
2.  **Deduct Remuneration:** Subtract any agreed-upon salaries, commissions, or fixed payments due to the working partner(s) from the total profit.
    *   `Remaining Profit = Total Profit - (Salaries + Commissions + Other Fixed Payments)`
3.  **Distribute Remaining Profit:** The remaining profit is then distributed among *all* partners (including both working and sleeping partners) according to their effective investment ratio (Investment × Time), as calculated in simple or compound partnership scenarios.
    *   Calculate the profit-sharing ratio based on `(I_A * T_A) : (I_B * T_B) : ...`
    *   Divide the `Remaining Profit` according to this ratio.
4.  **Calculate Total Earnings per Partner:**
    *   For a **working partner**, Total Earnings = Salary/Commission + Share of Remaining Profit.
    *   For a **sleeping partner**, Total Earnings = Share of Remaining Profit.

## Example Scenario

A and B are partners. A is a working partner entitled to a salary of ₹1000 per month. A invested ₹50k, B invested ₹100k. Total profit for the year is ₹80k.

1.  Total Profit = ₹80,000.
2.  A's total salary for the year = 1000 * 12 = ₹12,000.
3.  Remaining Profit = 80000 - 12000 = ₹68,000.
4.  Investment Ratio (Simple Partnership) = 50k : 100k = 1 : 2. Sum of parts = 3.
5.  A's share from remaining profit = (1/3) * 68000. B's share from remaining profit = (2/3) * 68000.
6.  A's Total Earnings = Salary + Share = 12000 + (1/3)*68000.
7.  B's Total Earnings = Share = (2/3)*68000.

## Key Considerations

*   The terms of remuneration (salary percentage, commission rate, fixed amount) must be clearly stated in the problem or partnership agreement.
*   Remuneration is deducted *before* the profit is shared based on investment ratios.
*   Losses are typically shared in the investment ratio, but partnership agreements can specify different terms. Salaries might not be paid if there's a net loss, depending on the agreement.
