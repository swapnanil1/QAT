# Partnership: Basic Concepts (Investment, Time, Profit Ratio)

#quant #partnership #basic_concepts #ratio_concepts #formula

## Definition

A **partnership** is an association of two or more persons who agree to invest their money, resources, or skills to run a business or project together and share the resulting profits or losses. The individuals involved are called **partners**.

## Key Components

1.  **Investment (Capital):** The amount of money or resources contributed by each partner. Let the investments of partners A, B, C be `I_A`, `I_B`, `I_C`, etc.
2.  **Time:** The duration for which each partner's investment remains in the business. Let the time periods be `T_A`, `T_B`, `T_C`, etc.
3.  **Profit/Loss:** The net gain or loss generated by the business over a period, which is to be distributed among the partners.

## Core Principle of Profit/Loss Sharing

The fundamental principle is that **profit (or loss) is distributed among partners in the ratio of their effective investments**, often called the **ratio of equivalent capitals**.

Equivalent Capital = Investment × Time Period

*   Ratio of Profits = Ratio of (Investment × Time)
*   If Profit_A, Profit_B, Profit_C are the shares of profit for partners A, B, C respectively:
    `Profit_A : Profit_B : Profit_C = (I_A * T_A) : (I_B * T_B) : (I_C * T_C)`

## Types of Partnership

1.  **Simple Partnership:**
    *   All partners invest their capital for the **same duration**.
    *   In this case, the time period `T` is common for all (`T_A = T_B = T_C = ... = T`).
    *   The profit-sharing ratio simplifies to the ratio of their investments only:
        `Profit_A : Profit_B : Profit_C = I_A : I_B : I_C`

2.  **Compound Partnership:**
    *   Partners invest their capital for **different durations**, or they invest different amounts for different periods within the overall partnership duration.
    *   The general formula must be used:
        `Profit_A : Profit_B : Profit_C = (I_A * T_A) : (I_B * T_B) : (I_C * T_C)`

## Types of Partners

1.  **Working Partner (Active Partner):** A partner who contributes capital *and* actively manages or works in the business. Working partners may sometimes receive a salary, commission, or an additional share of profit for their active role, *before* the remaining profit is distributed according to the investment ratios.
2.  **Sleeping Partner (Silent Partner / Dormant Partner):** A partner who contributes capital but does not actively participate in the management or operation of the business. Their profit share is based solely on their investment ratio unless otherwise specified in the agreement.

## Key Considerations

*   Ensure the **time periods** for all partners are calculated in the **same units** (e.g., months or years).
*   If a partner invests additional capital or withdraws capital during the period, their investment needs to be calculated as a sum of (Amount × Duration) for each phase of their investment.
*   Agreements regarding salaries or commissions for working partners must be accounted for *before* distributing the remaining profit based on the investment ratio.
