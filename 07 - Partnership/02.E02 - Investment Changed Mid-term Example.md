# Investment Changed Mid-term Example

Parent Concept: [[07 - Partnership/02 - Compound Partnership (Different Investment Times Amounts).md]]

## Problem:

Amit starts a business with ₹ 50,000. After 4 months, Bhavna joins him with ₹ 60,000. After another 4 months (i.e., 8 months from the start), Amit withdraws ₹ 20,000. If the total profit at the end of one year is ₹ 9,800, what is Bhavna's share in the profit?

(A) ₹ 4680
(B) ₹ 4704
(C) ₹ 5096
(D) ₹ 4800

## Solution:

**1. Identify the Type of Partnership:**
   This is a Compound Partnership as partners invest for different durations, and one partner changes their investment mid-term. Profit is shared based on Equivalent Capitals (Investment × Time).

**2. Determine the Total Duration:**
   The business runs for one year = 12 months.

**3. Calculate Amit's Equivalent Capital:**
   Amit's investment changes after 8 months.
   *   Period 1: Investment = ₹ 50,000. Duration = 8 months (from start until withdrawal).
       Contribution 1 = 50000 * 8 = 400000.
   *   Period 2: Investment = ₹ 50,000 - ₹ 20,000 = ₹ 30,000. Duration = Remaining 4 months (12 - 8).
       Contribution 2 = 30000 * 4 = 120000.
   *   Amit's Total Equivalent Capital = Contribution 1 + Contribution 2 = 400000 + 120000 = 520000.

**4. Calculate Bhavna's Equivalent Capital:**
   Bhavna joins after 4 months and invests ₹ 60,000 for the remaining period.
   *   Investment = ₹ 60,000.
   *   Duration = 12 months - 4 months = 8 months.
   *   Bhavna's Total Equivalent Capital = 60000 * 8 = 480000.

**5. Determine the Profit Sharing Ratio (Amit : Bhavna):**
   Ratio = Amit's Equivalent Capital : Bhavna's Equivalent Capital
   Ratio = 520000 : 480000

**6. Simplify the Ratio:**
   Divide by 10000:
   Ratio = 52 : 48
   Divide by their GCD, which is 4:
   Ratio = (52 / 4) : (48 / 4)
   Simplified Profit Sharing Ratio = 13 : 12

**7. Calculate the Sum of Ratio Parts:**
   Total parts = 13 + 12 = 25 parts.

**8. Calculate the Value of One Ratio Part:**
   Total Profit = ₹ 9,800.
   Value of 1 part = Total Profit / Total Parts
   Value of 1 part = 9800 / 25
   Value of 1 part = (9800 * 4) / (25 * 4) = 39200 / 100 = ₹ 392.

**9. Calculate Bhavna's Share of the Profit:**
   Bhavna's share corresponds to 12 parts.
   Bhavna's share = Bhavna's parts * Value of 1 part
   Bhavna's share = 12 * 392
   Bhavna's share = 12 * (400 - 8) = (12 * 400) - (12 * 8) = 4800 - 96 = 4704.

Therefore, Bhavna's share in the profit is ₹ 4704.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Timeline:** Visualizing the 12-month timeline helps track when investments start, change, and end for each partner. Mark months 0, 4, 8, 12.
*   **Sum of Products:** For partners with changing investments, calculate the sum of `(Investment × Time)` for each distinct period. Amit: (50k * 8) + (30k * 4).
*   **Consistent Units:** Use months consistently for all time calculations.
*   **Simplify Ratios:** Simplifying `520000 : 480000` to `13 : 12` significantly eases the final share calculation.
*   **Fraction Calculation:** To calculate `9800 / 25`, multiplying the numerator and denominator by 4 to get `/ 100` is often faster than direct long division.
