# Sleeping Partner Share Calculation Example

Parent Concept: [[07 - Partnership/03 - Working and Sleeping Partners.md]]

#quant #partnership #compound_partnership #profit_sharing #working_partner #sleeping_partner #salary #example

## Problem:

Ram (Working), Sam (Sleeping), and Tom (Sleeping) enter a partnership. Ram invests ₹30,000 for the whole year. Sam invests ₹40,000 for the whole year. Tom invests ₹60,000 but joins after 4 months. Ram manages the business and receives a salary of ₹1,000 per month. The remaining profit is shared according to their equivalent investments. If the total profit for the year is ₹ 67,000, what is Tom's share of the profit?

(A) ₹ 15,000
(B) ₹ 20,000
(C) ₹ 27,000
(D) ₹ 25,000

## Solution:

**1. Calculate the Working Partner's Salary:**
   Ram manages the business and receives a salary.
   Ram's total annual salary = ₹ 1,000/month * 12 months = ₹ 12,000.

**2. Calculate the Remaining Profit for Distribution:**
   Remaining Profit = Total Profit - Ram's Salary
   Remaining Profit = 67000 - 12000 = ₹ 55,000.
   This remaining profit is distributed among Ram, Sam, and Tom based on their investment contributions.

**3. Calculate the Equivalent Capital for each Partner:**
   Equivalent Capital = Investment × Time Period (in months)
   *   Ram: Invested ₹30,000 for 12 months.
       Equivalent Capital (Ram) = 30000 * 12 = 360000.
   *   Sam: Invested ₹40,000 for 12 months.
       Equivalent Capital (Sam) = 40000 * 12 = 480000.
   *   Tom: Invested ₹60,000 after 4 months, so for 12 - 4 = 8 months.
       Equivalent Capital (Tom) = 60000 * 8 = 480000.

**4. Determine the Profit Sharing Ratio (Ram : Sam : Tom):**
   Ratio = Equivalent Capital (Ram) : Equivalent Capital (Sam) : Equivalent Capital (Tom)
   Ratio = `360000 : 480000 : 480000`

**5. Simplify the Ratio:**
   Divide by 10000:
   Ratio = `36 : 48 : 48`
   Divide by their GCD, which is 12:
   Ratio = `(36/12) : (48/12) : (48/12)`
   Simplified Profit Sharing Ratio = `3 : 4 : 4`

**6. Calculate the Sum of Ratio Parts:**
   Total parts = 3 + 4 + 4 = 11 parts.

**7. Distribute the Remaining Profit:**
   The remaining profit of ₹ 55,000 is distributed in the ratio 3 : 4 : 4.
   Value of 1 part = Remaining Profit / Total Parts
   Value of 1 part = 55000 / 11 = ₹ 5,000.

**8. Calculate Tom's Share of the Profit:**
   Tom is a sleeping partner. His earnings come solely from the distribution of the remaining profit based on his investment ratio.
   Tom's share corresponds to 4 parts in the ratio.
   Tom's share = Tom's ratio part * Value of 1 part
   Tom's share = 4 * 5000 = 20000.

Therefore, Tom's share of the profit is ₹ 20,000.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Isolate Remuneration:** Mentally (or physically) separate the working partner's fixed salary/commission from the total profit pool first.
*   **Focus on Remainder:** Remember that the investment ratio (3:4:4 here) only applies to the profit *after* the salary has been paid (₹ 55,000).
*   **Sleeping Partner Calculation:** For a sleeping partner like Tom, the calculation is straightforward once the value per ratio part (based on the remaining profit) is found. Their share is simply `(Their Ratio Part) * (Value per Part)`. They don't get the salary component.
*   **Compound Calculation:** Don't forget to calculate equivalent capitals correctly when partners invest for different durations (like Tom investing for only 8 months).
