## Problem:

Anil, Ben, and Charles start a business together. Anil invests ₹ 25,000, Ben invests ₹ 30,000, and Charles invests ₹ 45,000. They all invested for the entire year. At the end of the year, they earned a total profit of ₹ 12,000. What is the difference between Charles's share and Anil's share of the profit?

(A) ₹ 2000
(B) ₹ 2400
(C) ₹ 3000
(D) ₹ 3600

## Solution:

**1. Identify the Type of Partnership:**
   Since Anil, Ben, and Charles invested their capital for the same duration (one year), this is a case of Simple Partnership.

**2. Determine the Profit Sharing Ratio:**
   In a simple partnership, the profit is shared in the ratio of the investments.
   Investment Ratio (Anil : Ben : Charles) = `25000 : 30000 : 45000`

**3. Simplify the Investment Ratio:**
   Divide all terms by their greatest common divisor. We can start by dividing by 1000:
   Ratio = `25 : 30 : 45`
   Now, divide all terms by their GCD, which is 5:
   Ratio = `(25/5) : (30/5) : (45/5)`
   Simplified Profit Sharing Ratio (Anil : Ben : Charles) = `5 : 6 : 9`

**4. Calculate the Sum of Ratio Parts:**
   Total parts = 5 + 6 + 9 = 20 parts.

**5. Calculate the Value of One Ratio Part:**
   Total Profit = ₹ 12,000.
   Value of 1 part = Total Profit / Total Parts
   Value of 1 part = 12000 / 20
   Value of 1 part = 1200 / 2 = ₹ 600.

**6. Calculate the Required Difference (Charles's share - Anil's share):**
   *Method 1: Calculate individual shares.*
     Anil's share = 5 parts * 600 = ₹ 3000.
     Charles's share = 9 parts * 600 = ₹ 5400.
     Difference = Charles's share - Anil's share = 5400 - 3000 = ₹ 2400.

   *Method 2: Calculate the difference in ratio parts first.*
     Difference in parts = Charles's parts - Anil's parts = 9 - 5 = 4 parts.
     Difference amount = Difference in parts * Value of 1 part
     Difference amount = 4 * 600 = ₹ 2400.

Therefore, the difference between Charles's share and Anil's share is ₹ 2400.

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Identify Simple Partnership:** If investment durations are equal, the profit ratio is simply the investment ratio. Ignore the time component.
*   **Simplify Ratios:** Simplify the investment ratio as much as possible before calculating shares. Dividing by common factors (like 1000 and then 5) makes the numbers smaller and calculations easier.
*   **Calculate Difference Directly:** If only the difference between shares is asked, find the difference between their corresponding ratio parts (9 - 5 = 4 parts) and multiply by the value of one part (4 * 600 = 2400). This is faster than finding individual shares.
*   **Verification:** Quickly check total profit: Anil(3000) + Ben(6*600=3600) + Charles(5400) = 3000 + 3600 + 5400 = 12000. Matches the given total profit.
