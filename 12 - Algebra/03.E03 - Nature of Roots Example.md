# Nature of Roots Example

**Problem:**
Without solving the equation, determine the nature of the roots of the quadratic equation `3x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0`.

(A) Two distinct real roots
(B) Two equal real roots (One real root)
(C) No real roots (Two complex conjugate roots)
(D) Cannot be determined without solving

## Solution:

This problem asks for the *nature* of the roots, not their exact values. We can determine this by calculating the discriminant (`Δ`) of the quadratic equation, as explained in [[12 - Algebra/03 - Quadratic Equations.md]].

The standard form of a quadratic equation is `ax^2 + bx + c = 0`.

**Step 1: Identify Coefficients**
For the equation `3x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0`:
*   `a = 3`
*   `b = -5`
*   `c = 4`

**Step 2: Calculate the Discriminant (Δ)**
The discriminant formula is `Δ = b^2 - 4ac`.
Substitute the values of `a`, `b`, and `c`:
`Δ = (-5)^2 - 4 * (3) * (4)`
`Δ = 25 - 4 * 12`
`Δ = 25 - 48`
`Δ = -23`

**Step 3: Analyze the Discriminant**
The value of the discriminant is `Δ = -23`.
According to the rules for the discriminant:
*   If `Δ > 0`, there are two distinct real roots.
*   If `Δ = 0`, there are two equal real roots.
*   If `Δ < 0`, there are no real roots (the roots are complex conjugates).

Since `Δ = -23`, which is less than 0 (`Δ < 0`), the equation has no real roots.

**Step 4: State the Nature of the Roots**
The quadratic equation `3x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0` has no real roots (or two complex conjugate roots). This corresponds to option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip
1.  **Focus on Discriminant:** When asked only about the *nature* of the roots (real, equal, distinct, complex), the discriminant `Δ = b^2 - 4ac` is all you need to calculate. Don't waste time trying to solve the equation using the full quadratic formula or factoring.
2.  **Sign Precision:** Be very careful when calculating `b^2` (especially if `b` is negative, `(-b)^2` is positive) and the sign of the `-4ac` term. An error here will lead to the wrong conclusion about the nature of the roots.
3.  **Interpret Δ Correctly:** Memorize the three conditions: `Δ > 0` (2 distinct real), `Δ = 0` (1 real / 2 equal real), `Δ < 0` (no real / 2 complex).
