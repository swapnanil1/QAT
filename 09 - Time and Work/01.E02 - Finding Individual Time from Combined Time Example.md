# Finding Individual Time from Combined Time Example

**Problem:**

A and B working together can finish a certain piece of work in 8 days. If A alone can finish the same work in 12 days, in how many days can B alone finish the work?

(A) 16 days
(B) 20 days
(C) 24 days
(D) 30 days

## Solution:

We need to find the time B takes alone, given the time A takes alone and the time they take working together. The LCM method from [[09 - Time and Work/01 - Basic Concepts (Work Rate, LCM Method).md]] is suitable here.

**Step 1: Assume Total Work using LCM**
Given times are 8 days (A+B) and 12 days (A).
Find the LCM of the time periods: LCM(8, 12).
Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32,...
Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36,...
The LCM is 24.
Let the Total Work = 24 units.

**Step 2: Calculate Efficiencies**
Efficiency = Total Work / Time Taken

*   Efficiency of A (`Eff_A`) = Total Work / Time taken by A = 24 units / 12 days = 2 units/day.
*   Combined Efficiency of A and B (`Eff_A+B`) = Total Work / Time taken by A and B = 24 units / 8 days = 3 units/day.

**Step 3: Calculate B's Efficiency**
The combined efficiency is the sum of individual efficiencies:
`Eff_A+B = Eff_A + Eff_B`
`3 = 2 + Eff_B`
Subtract `Eff_A` from the combined efficiency to find `Eff_B`:
`Eff_B = Eff_A+B - Eff_A`
`Eff_B = 3 units/day - 2 units/day = 1 unit/day`.

**Step 4: Calculate Time Taken by B Alone**
Time Taken by B = Total Work / Efficiency of B
Time Taken by B = 24 units / 1 unit/day = 24 days.

So, B alone can finish the work in 24 days. This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

1.  **Fraction Method Formula:** If A takes `x` days, B takes `y` days, and together they take `t` days, the relationship is `1/x + 1/y = 1/t`. We need to find `y`.
    `1/y = 1/t - 1/x`
    `1/y = (x - t) / (xt)`
    `y = xt / (x - t)`

    Substitute the given values: `x = 12`, `t = 8`.
    `y = (12 * 8) / (12 - 8)`
    `y = 96 / 4`
    `y = 24` days.

    This formula `y = xt / (x - t)` (or `x = yt / (y - t)`) is a direct shortcut when one individual time and the combined time are given, and you need to find the other individual time.

2.  **Efficiency Logic:**
    *   Together (A+B) do 1/8 of the work per day.
    *   A alone does 1/12 of the work per day.
    *   Therefore, B alone does `(1/8) - (1/12)` of the work per day.
    *   `1/8 - 1/12 = (3 - 2) / 24 = 1/24` (Work done by B per day).
    *   If B does 1/24 of the work per day, B will take 24 days to complete the work.

The formula `y = xt / (x - t)` is the fastest approach for this specific type of MCQ.
