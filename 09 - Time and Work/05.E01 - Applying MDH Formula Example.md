# Applying MDH Formula Example

**Problem:**

If 15 men, working 8 hours a day, can complete 2/3 of a project in 20 days, how many *additional* men must be employed to finish the remaining work in the next 10 days, if everyone now works 6 hours a day?

(A) 20
(B) 10
(C) 5
(D) 8

## Solution:

This problem requires the application of the Men-Days-Hours-Work formula (MDH formula) discussed in [[09 - Time and Work/05 - Men-Days-Hours Formula (M1D1H1 W1 = M2D2H2 W2).md]].

The formula is: `(M1 * D1 * H1) / W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2) / W2`

**Identify the parameters for both scenarios:**

**Scenario 1 (Given):**
*   Number of men, `M1 = 15`
*   Number of days, `D1 = 20`
*   Hours per day, `H1 = 8`
*   Work done, `W1 = 2/3` (of the project)

**Scenario 2 (To find M2):**
*   Number of men, `M2 = ?` (Let this be the total number of men required in the second phase)
*   Number of days, `D2 = 10`
*   Hours per day, `H2 = 6`
*   Work done, `W2 = 1 - W1 = 1 - 2/3 = 1/3` (the remaining work)

**Apply the formula:**

`(15 * 20 * 8) / (2/3) = (M2 * 10 * 6) / (1/3)`

**Solve for M2:**

To simplify, multiply both sides by their denominators' reciprocals:
`(15 * 20 * 8) * (3/2) = (M2 * 10 * 6) * (3/1)`

Now, simplify both sides:
Left Side: `15 * (20/2) * 8 * 3 = 15 * 10 * 8 * 3 = 120 * 10 * 3 = 1200 * 3 = 3600`
Right Side: `M2 * 10 * 6 * 3 = M2 * 180`

So, the equation becomes:
`3600 = M2 * 180`

Divide both sides by 180:
`M2 = 3600 / 180`
`M2 = 360 / 18`
`M2 = 20`

This means a total of 20 men are required for the second phase (completing the remaining work under the new conditions).

**Find the number of additional men:**

The question asks for the number of *additional* men needed.
Original number of men = M1 = 15
Total men required in phase 2 = M2 = 20
Additional men = M2 - M1 = 20 - 15 = 5.

Therefore, 5 additional men must be employed. This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

1.  **Set up the MDH Equation:** Correctly identify M1, D1, H1, W1 and M2, D2, H2, W2. Crucially, note that W2 is the *remaining* work (1 - W1).
2.  **Simplify Before Multiplying:** Instead of calculating large numbers like 3600, simplify the equation by canceling common factors *after* cross-multiplying or rearranging.
    `(15 * 20 * 8) / (2/3) = (M2 * 10 * 6) / (1/3)`
    `(15 * 20 * 8 * 3) / 2 = (M2 * 10 * 6 * 3) / 1`
    Cancel 3 from both numerators:
    `(15 * 20 * 8) / 2 = M2 * 10 * 6`
    Cancel 10 from 20 on the left and 10 on the right:
    `(15 * 2 * 8) / 2 = M2 * 6`
    Cancel 2 from the numerator and denominator on the left:
    `15 * 8 = M2 * 6`
    `120 = M2 * 6`
    `M2 = 20`.
3.  **Answer the Exact Question:** Double-check if the question asks for the total number of workers (M2) or the *additional* workers (M2 - M1). Option (A) is a common distractor representing M2.
