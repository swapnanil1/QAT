# A and B Working on Alternate Days Example

**Problem:**

A can complete a piece of work in 9 days, and B can complete the same work in 12 days. If they work on alternate days starting with A, in how many days will the work be completed?

(A) 10 1/4 days
(B) 10 1/3 days
(C) 10 days
(D) 11 days

## Solution:

This problem involves two individuals working on alternate days, starting with A. We will use the LCM method and the cycle approach described in [[09 - Time and Work/03 - Alternate Days Work.md]].

**Step 1: Assume Total Work using LCM**
Time taken by A = 9 days
Time taken by B = 12 days
LCM(9, 12) = 36.
Let Total Work = 36 units.

**Step 2: Calculate Individual Efficiencies**
Efficiency of A (`Eff_A`) = Total Work / Time_A = 36 / 9 = 4 units/day.
Efficiency of B (`Eff_B`) = Total Work / Time_B = 36 / 12 = 3 units/day.

**Step 3: Analyze the Work Cycle (2 days)**
A starts the work.
*   Day 1 (A works): 4 units done.
*   Day 2 (B works): 3 units done.
*   Total work done in one cycle of 2 days = `Eff_A + Eff_B` = 4 + 3 = 7 units.

**Step 4: Calculate Number of Full Cycles**
We need to complete 36 units of work.
Divide the Total Work by the work done per cycle: 36 / 7.
36 ÷ 7 = 5 with a remainder of 1.
So, 5 full cycles can be completed.

*   Work done in 5 cycles = 5 cycles * 7 units/cycle = 35 units.
*   Time taken for 5 cycles = 5 cycles * 2 days/cycle = 10 days.

**Step 5: Calculate Remaining Work**
Remaining Work = Total Work - Work Done in Cycles
Remaining Work = 36 - 35 = 1 unit.

**Step 6: Complete Remaining Work**
After 10 days (which completes 5 cycles), the 11th day begins.
Since A started the work, A works on Day 1, Day 3, ..., Day 11.
It is A's turn to work on the 11th day.
A's efficiency is 4 units/day.
The remaining work is 1 unit.
Time needed by A to complete the remaining work = Remaining Work / Eff_A = 1 unit / 4 units/day = 1/4 day.

**Step 7: Calculate Total Time**
Total Time = Time for Full Cycles + Time for Remaining Work
Total Time = 10 days + 1/4 day = 10 1/4 days.

The work will be completed in 10 1/4 days. This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip

1.  **Calculate Efficiencies and Work per Cycle:** Find individual efficiencies (A=4, B=3) and work per 2-day cycle (A+B = 7).
2.  **Find Full Cycles:** Divide Total Work (36) by Work per Cycle (7). `36 / 7 = 5` full cycles.
3.  **Time for Cycles:** Time = `5 cycles * 2 days/cycle = 10 days`.
4.  **Work Done & Remaining:** Work done = `5 * 7 = 35 units`. Remaining = `36 - 35 = 1 unit`.
5.  **Whose Turn?** After an even number of days (10 days), the person who started (A) works next.
6.  **Time for Remainder:** Time = Remaining Work / Next Person's Efficiency = `1 / 4` day.
7.  **Total Time:** 10 + 1/4 = 10 1/4 days.

Always check who starts, as this determines who works on the day *after* the full cycles are complete. If B had started, B would work on Day 11, and the time for the remainder would be `1 / 3` day, leading to option (B) as a distractor.
