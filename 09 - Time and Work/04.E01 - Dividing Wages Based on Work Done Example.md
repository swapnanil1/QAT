# Dividing Wages Based on Work Done Example

**Problem:**

A can do a piece of work in 6 days, B in 8 days, and C in 12 days. They work together to complete the work and earn Rs. 2340 for the entire job. What is B's share of the earnings?

(A) Rs. 780
(B) Rs. 630
(C) Rs. 540
(D) Rs. 1170

## Solution:

According to the principle outlined in [[09 - Time and Work/04 - Work and Wages.md]], wages are distributed in proportion to the work done. When individuals work together for the entire duration to complete a task, the ratio of work done is equal to the ratio of their efficiencies.

**Step 1: Assume Total Work using LCM**
Time taken by A = 6 days
Time taken by B = 8 days
Time taken by C = 12 days
LCM(6, 8, 12) = 24.
Let Total Work = 24 units.

**Step 2: Calculate Individual Efficiencies**
Efficiency = Total Work / Time Taken
Efficiency of A (`Eff_A`) = 24 / 6 = 4 units/day.
Efficiency of B (`Eff_B`) = 24 / 8 = 3 units/day.
Efficiency of C (`Eff_C`) = 24 / 12 = 2 units/day.

**Step 3: Determine the Ratio of Work Done / Efficiencies**
Since they work together for the entire duration, the ratio of work done by A, B, and C is equal to the ratio of their efficiencies:
Ratio (A : B : C) = `Eff_A : Eff_B : Eff_C` = `4 : 3 : 2`.

**Step 4: Calculate the Sum of Ratio Parts**
Sum of ratio parts = 4 + 3 + 2 = 9.

**Step 5: Calculate B's Share of the Wages**
Total Wages = Rs. 2340.
B's share corresponds to his part in the ratio (3 parts out of 9 total parts).
B's Share = `(B's ratio part / Sum of ratio parts) * Total Wages`
B's Share = `(3 / 9) * 2340`
B's Share = `(1 / 3) * 2340`
B's Share = 2340 / 3 = 780.

B's share of the earnings is Rs. 780. This matches option (A).

Let's check the other shares (optional):
A's Share = (4 / 9) * 2340 = 4 * (2340 / 9) = 4 * 260 = Rs. 1040.
C's Share = (2 / 9) * 2340 = 2 * (2340 / 9) = 2 * 260 = Rs. 520.
Total = 1040 + 780 + 520 = 2340. (Correct)

## Shortcut/Tip

1.  **Find Efficiencies:** Quickly find the efficiencies using LCM. A: 24/6=4, B: 24/8=3, C: 24/12=2.
2.  **Ratio is Key:** Recognise that the efficiency ratio (4:3:2) is directly the wage ratio because they worked together throughout.
3.  **Targeted Calculation:** Focus only on the required share (B's). B's fraction is `3 / (4+3+2) = 3/9 = 1/3`.
4.  **Calculate Share:** B's Share = `(1/3) * Total Wages` = `2340 / 3 = 780`.

This avoids calculating the time taken together or the shares of other workers, saving time in an MCQ context.
