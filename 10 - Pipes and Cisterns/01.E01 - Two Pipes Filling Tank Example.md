# Two Pipes Filling Tank Example

**Problem:**

Two pipes A and B can fill a tank completely in 12 hours and 18 hours respectively. If both pipes are opened simultaneously, how much time will it take to fill the tank completely?

(A) 7.2 hours
(B) 7.5 hours
(C) 15 hours
(D) 6 hours

## Solution:

This is a standard problem involving two inlets working together. We can use the LCM method (Capacity Method) described in [[10 - Pipes and Cisterns/01 - Basic Concepts (Filling Emptying Rates, LCM Method).md]].

**Step 1: Assume Tank Capacity using LCM**
Time taken by Pipe A to fill = 12 hours.
Time taken by Pipe B to fill = 18 hours.
LCM(12, 18) = 36.
Let the Total Capacity of the tank = 36 units.

**Step 2: Calculate Individual Efficiencies (Filling Rates)**
Efficiency = Capacity / Time Taken
Efficiency of A (`Eff_A`) = 36 units / 12 hours = +3 units/hour. (Positive as it's filling)
Efficiency of B (`Eff_B`) = 36 units / 18 hours = +2 units/hour. (Positive as it's filling)

**Step 3: Calculate Combined Efficiency**
When both pipes are opened, their efficiencies add up:
Combined Efficiency (`Eff_A+B`) = `Eff_A + Eff_B` = 3 units/hour + 2 units/hour = 5 units/hour.

**Step 4: Calculate Time Taken Together**
Time to fill together = Total Capacity / Combined Efficiency
Time to fill together = 36 units / 5 units/hour
Time to fill together = 36/5 hours.

Converting 36/5 hours to decimal:
36 / 5 = 7.2 hours.

*(Optional: Converting to hours and minutes: 7.2 hours = 7 hours + 0.2 hours = 7 hours + (0.2 * 60) minutes = 7 hours + 12 minutes)*

It will take 7.2 hours to fill the tank completely when both pipes are open. This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip

For problems involving only two pipes (or workers in Time & Work) working together, a direct formula can be used, derived from the fraction method:

`Time (A+B) = (Time_A * Time_B) / (Time_A + Time_B)`

Where `Time_A` and `Time_B` are the times taken by A and B individually.

Applying the formula:
`Time (A+B) = (12 * 18) / (12 + 18)`
`Time (A+B) = (12 * 18) / 30`

Simplify by dividing 12 and 30 by 6:
`Time (A+B) = (2 * 18) / 5`
`Time (A+B) = 36 / 5 = 7.2` hours.

This formula is faster than setting up the full LCM method for the simple case of two inlets.
