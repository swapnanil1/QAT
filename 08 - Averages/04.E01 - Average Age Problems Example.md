# Average Age Problems Example

**Problem:**

The average age of 30 students in a class is 14 years. When the age of the class teacher is included, the average age becomes 15 years. What is the age of the class teacher?

(A) 40 years
(B) 44 years
(C) 45 years
(D) 46 years

## Solution:

This problem involves finding the value (age) of a person added to a group, which changes the average. We can apply the concepts from [[08 - Averages/04 - Problems on Ages, Runs, etc.md]] and [[08 - Averages/02 - Properties of Averages.md]].

**Method 1: Using Total Sum**

1.  **Calculate the initial total age of the students.**
    Number of students = 30
    Average age of students = 14 years
    Total age of students = Average * Number = 14 * 30 = 420 years.

2.  **Calculate the new total age after including the teacher.**
    New number of people (students + teacher) = 30 + 1 = 31
    New average age = 15 years
    New total age = New Average * New Number = 15 * 31

    Calculation: 15 * 31 = 15 * (30 + 1) = (15 * 30) + (15 * 1) = 450 + 15 = 465 years.

3.  **Find the age of the teacher.**
    The teacher's age is the difference between the new total age and the initial total age.
    Teacher's Age = New Total Age - Initial Total Age
    Teacher's Age = 465 - 420 = 45 years.

The age of the class teacher is 45 years. This matches option (C).

**Method 2: Using Change in Average (Shortcut)**

1.  **Identify the change in average:** The average increased from 14 years to 15 years.
    Change in Average = 15 - 14 = +1 year.
2.  **Identify the new number of people:** 30 students + 1 teacher = 31 people.
3.  **Calculate the total increase due to the teacher:** The teacher's age must be such that it increases the average of all 31 people by 1 year compared to the original average.
    Total Increase = Change in Average * New Number of People
    Total Increase = 1 * 31 = 31 years.
4.  **Calculate the teacher's age:** The teacher's age must be 31 years *more* than the original average age.
    Teacher's Age = Original Average + Total Increase
    Teacher's Age = 14 + 31 = 45 years.

## Shortcut/Tip

Method 2 is significantly faster for MCQs:

1.  **Increase in Average:** 15 - 14 = 1 year.
2.  **Final Count:** 30 + 1 = 31 people.
3.  **Teacher's Age = Old Average + (Increase in Avg * Final Count)**
    Teacher's Age = 14 + (1 * 31) = 14 + 31 = 45 years.

This directly calculates the added person's value based on how they shifted the overall average.
