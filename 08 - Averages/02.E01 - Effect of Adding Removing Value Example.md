# Effect of Adding/Removing Value Example

**Problem:**

The average age of a class of 15 students is 18 years. When the age of the teacher is included, the average age increases to 18.5 years. What is the age of the teacher?

(A) 25.5 years
(B) 26.0 years
(C) 26.5 years
(D) 18.5 years

## Solution:

This problem demonstrates the effect of adding a new value (the teacher's age) to a group, as described in [[08 - Averages/02 - Properties of Averages.md]]. We can use the relationship `Sum = Average * Number of Observations`.

**Method 1: Using Total Sum**

1.  **Calculate the initial total age of the students.**
    Number of students = 15
    Average age of students = 18 years
    Total age of students = Average * Number = 18 * 15 = 270 years.

2.  **Calculate the new total age after including the teacher.**
    New number of people (students + teacher) = 15 + 1 = 16
    New average age = 18.5 years
    New total age = New Average * New Number = 18.5 * 16

    Calculation: 18.5 * 16 = 18.5 * (10 + 6) = (18.5 * 10) + (18.5 * 6) = 185 + (18 * 6 + 0.5 * 6) = 185 + (108 + 3) = 185 + 111 = 296 years.

3.  **Find the age of the teacher.**
    The difference between the new total age (including the teacher) and the initial total age (students only) must be the teacher's age.
    Teacher's Age = New Total Age - Initial Total Age
    Teacher's Age = 296 - 270 = 26 years.

The age of the teacher is 26 years. This matches option (B).

**Method 2: Using Properties of Averages (Conceptual Shortcut)**

1.  If the teacher's age was exactly the same as the students' average age (18 years), including the teacher would not change the average.
2.  However, the average increased from 18 to 18.5 years, an increase of 0.5 years.
3.  This increase of 0.5 years applies to *all* 16 people (15 students + 1 teacher).
4.  The *total increase* in the sum of ages caused by the teacher being older than 18 is:
    Total Increase = Increase in Average * New Number of People
    Total Increase = 0.5 * 16 = 8 years.
5.  This means the teacher's age must be 8 years *more* than the original average age.
    Teacher's Age = Original Average + Total Increase
    Teacher's Age = 18 + 8 = 26 years.

## Shortcut/Tip

The quickest way to solve this in an MCQ setting is Method 2:

1.  **Find the change in average:** New Average - Old Average = 18.5 - 18 = +0.5 years.
2.  **Find the new total count:** Old Count + 1 = 15 + 1 = 16 people.
3.  **Calculate the total deviation:** Change in Average * New Count = 0.5 * 16 = 8 years. This represents the "extra" age the new person brings compared to the original average.
4.  **Calculate the new person's value:** Original Average + Total Deviation = 18 + 8 = 26 years.

*If the average had decreased*, the change would be negative, and you would add a negative deviation (i.e., subtract) from the original average to find the age of the person added (or the person removed if the problem was about removing someone).
