# Simple Average Calculation Example (Data Correction)

**Problem:**

The average weight of a group of 25 students was calculated to be 50 kg. It was later discovered that the weight of one student was misread as 45 kg instead of his actual weight of 70 kg. What is the correct average weight of the group?

(A) 50.0 kg
(B) 50.5 kg
(C) 51.0 kg
(D) 49.0 kg

## Solution:

We will use the relationship `Sum = Average * Number of Observations` from the [[08 - Averages/01 - Basic Average Formula.md]].

**Step 1: Calculate the initial (incorrect) total weight.**
Number of students = 25
Incorrect Average = 50 kg

Incorrect Total Weight = Incorrect Average * Number of Students
Incorrect Total Weight = 50 * 25 = 1250 kg

**Step 2: Find the error in the recorded weight.**
Incorrect weight recorded = 45 kg
Actual correct weight = 70 kg
Difference = Actual Weight - Incorrect Weight
Difference = 70 - 45 = 25 kg

This means the calculated total weight (1250 kg) is 25 kg less than the actual total weight.

**Step 3: Calculate the correct total weight.**
Correct Total Weight = Incorrect Total Weight + Difference
Correct Total Weight = 1250 + 25 = 1275 kg

**Step 4: Calculate the correct average weight.**
Correct Average = Correct Total Weight / Number of Students
Correct Average = 1275 / 25

To perform the division: 1275 / 25
We know 1250 / 25 = 50.
The remaining part is 25 / 25 = 1.
So, 1275 / 25 = 50 + 1 = 51 kg.

The correct average weight of the group is 51.0 kg. This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

Avoid calculating the full sum. Focus directly on the impact of the error on the average.

1.  **Calculate the difference** in the single incorrect observation: 70 kg (Correct) - 45 kg (Incorrect) = +25 kg. This is the net change needed for the *total sum*.
2.  **Distribute this change** across all observations to find the change in the *average*:
    Change in Average = Total Difference / Number of Observations
    Change in Average = 25 kg / 25 students = 1 kg.
3.  **Adjust the original average:** Since the correct weight is higher, the average must increase.
    Correct Average = Original Average + Change in Average
    Correct Average = 50 kg + 1 kg = 51 kg.

This approach bypasses the larger sum calculation (1250 and 1275) and directly computes the adjustment (1 kg) to the average, making it much faster for MCQs.
