# Basic Percentage Concepts and Conversions

Tags: #quant #percentages #basic_concepts #conversions

## Definition

The term "percent" comes from the Latin phrase "per centum," meaning "per hundred" or "for every hundred." A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. It is denoted by the symbol "%".

Essentially, x% means x hundredths, or x/100.

## Key Conversions

Understanding how to convert between percentages, fractions, and decimals is crucial for solving problems quickly.

### 1. Percentage to Fraction

Divide the percentage value by 100 and simplify the resulting fraction.
**Formula:** x% = x / 100

*   **Example:** Convert 45% to a fraction.
    45% = 45 / 100 = 9 / 20
*   **Example (Mixed Percent):** Convert 16 2/3 % to a fraction.
    16 2/3 % = (50/3) % = (50/3) / 100 = 50 / 300 = 1 / 6

### 2. Fraction to Percentage

Multiply the fraction by 100 and add the % symbol.
**Formula:** (a/b) = (a/b) * 100 %

*   **Example:** Convert 3/8 to a percentage.
    (3/8) * 100 % = 300/8 % = 75/2 % = 37.5%
*   **Example (Improper Fraction):** Convert 5/4 to a percentage.
    (5/4) * 100 % = 500/4 % = 125%

### 3. Percentage to Decimal

Divide the percentage value by 100 (or simply move the decimal point two places to the left).
**Formula:** x% = x / 100

*   **Example:** Convert 65% to a decimal.
    65% = 65 / 100 = 0.65
*   **Example:** Convert 8% to a decimal.
    8% = 8 / 100 = 0.08
*   **Example:** Convert 150% to a decimal.
    150% = 150 / 100 = 1.5

### 4. Decimal to Percentage

Multiply the decimal by 100 (or simply move the decimal point two places to the right) and add the % symbol.
**Formula:** d = d * 100 %

*   **Example:** Convert 0.75 to a percentage.
    0.75 * 100 % = 75%
*   **Example:** Convert 0.05 to a percentage.
    0.05 * 100 % = 5%
*   **Example:** Convert 1.2 to a percentage.
    1.2 * 100 % = 120%

## Basic Percentage Calculations

### 1. Finding 'x%' of a number 'y'

Multiply the number 'y' by the percentage 'x' expressed as a fraction or decimal.
**Formula:** x% of y = (x / 100) * y

*   **Example:** What is 40% of 150?
    (40 / 100) * 150 = 0.4 * 150 = 60
    *Alternatively (using fractions):* (2/5) * 150 = 2 * 30 = 60

### 2. Finding what percentage 'x' is of 'y'

Divide 'x' by 'y' and multiply the result by 100. The number you are comparing *to* (the 'of' number) goes in the denominator.
**Formula:** (x / y) * 100 %

*   **Example:** What percentage of 80 is 20?
    (20 / 80) * 100 % = (1/4) * 100 % = 25%

## Shortcut: Important Fractional Equivalents

Memorizing common percentage-fraction equivalents significantly speeds up calculations.

| Fraction | Percentage        | Fraction | Percentage       | Fraction | Percentage   |
| :------- | :---------------- | :------- | :--------------- | :------- | :----------- |
| 1/1      | 100%              | 1/8      | 12.5%            | 1/15     | 6.66...%     |
| 1/2      | 50%               | 1/9      | 11.11...%        | 1/16     | 6.25%        |
| 1/3      | 33.33...%         | 1/10     | 10%              | 1/20     | 5%           |
| 1/4      | 25%               | 1/11     | 9.09...%         | 1/25     | 4%           |
| 1/5      | 20%               | 1/12     | 8.33...%         | 1/50     | 2%           |
| 1/6      | 16.66...% (16 2/3%) | 1/13     | 7.69...%         | 3/8      | 37.5%        |
| 1/7      | 14.28...% (14 2/7%) | 1/14     | 7.14...% (7 1/7%) | 2/3      | 66.66...%    |

**Tip:** Use these equivalents whenever possible instead of performing full calculations. For example, finding 37.5% of 80 becomes (3/8) * 80 = 3 * 10 = 30, which is much faster than (37.5 / 100) * 80.
