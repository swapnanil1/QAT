# Example: Fraction to Percentage Conversion

Tags: #quant #percentages #basic_concepts #conversions #example #fraction_to_percentage

Parent Concept: [[02 - Percentages/01 - Basic Concepts and Conversions]]

## Problem

A solution contains 7 parts water and 5 parts alcohol. What percentage of the solution is alcohol?

## Solution

1.  **Find the total parts:** The total number of parts in the solution is the sum of water parts and alcohol parts.
    Total Parts = 7 (water) + 5 (alcohol) = 12 parts

2.  **Form the fraction:** The fraction representing alcohol in the solution is the number of alcohol parts divided by the total number of parts.
    Fraction of Alcohol = (Parts of Alcohol) / (Total Parts) = 5 / 12

3.  **Convert the fraction to a percentage:** Multiply the fraction by 100%.
    Percentage of Alcohol = (5 / 12) * 100 %
    = 500 / 12 %
    = 125 / 3 %
    = 41.66... % or 41 2/3 %

## Shortcut/Tip

*   Recognise that 1/12 is equivalent to 8.33...% (or 8 1/3 %).
*   Therefore, 5/12 = 5 * (1/12) = 5 * 8.33...% ≈ 41.67%
*   Or, 5/12 = 5 * (8 1/3 %) = 5 * (25/3 %) = 125/3 % = 41 2/3 %

**Answer:** Alcohol constitutes 41 2/3 % (or approximately 41.67%) of the solution.
