# Example: LCM using Common Division Method

**Tags:** #quant #number_system #lcm #example #common_division

**Concept:** [[03 - HCF and LCM]]

---

**Problem:**
Find the LCM of 16, 24, and 36 using the Common Division method.

**Method Applied:**
1.  Arrange the numbers horizontally.
2.  Divide the numbers by the smallest prime number that divides at least two of them. Write the quotients and any undivided numbers below.
3.  Repeat the process with the new row of numbers.
4.  Continue until the numbers in the row are all 1 or are pairwise co-prime (no two numbers share a common factor other than 1).
5.  The LCM is the product of all the prime divisors used on the left and any remaining numbers in the final row.

**Solution:**

Here's the step-by-step division process:

```
2 | 16   24   36
--|---------------  
2 | 8 12 18 <- (All divisible by 2)
--|---------------  
2 | 4 6 9 <- (8, 12, 18 divisible by 2)
--|---------------  
2 | 2 3 9 <- (4, 6 divisible by 2; 9 carried down)
--|---------------  
2 | 1 3 9 <- (2 divisible by 2 -> 1; 3, 9 carried down) 
--|---------------  
3 | 1 1 3 <- (3, 9 divisible by 3) 
--|---------------  
3 | 1 1 1 <- (3/3) (3 divisible by 3 -> 1)
```

```
The prime divisors used are 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3.
The remaining numbers in the last row are all 1.

LCM = 2 * 2 * 2 * 2 * 3 * 3
LCM = 16 * 9
LCM = 144

**Conclusion:**
The LCM of 16, 24, and 36 is **144**.
```
## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Systematic Division:** Using prime divisors (2, 3, 5, 7...) sequentially ensures you don't miss factors and arrive at the *least* common multiple. Divide by a prime as long as it divides at least one number in the current row.
*   **Carry Down:** Carefully carry down numbers that are not divisible in each step. Errors often occur here.
*   **Final Product:** Multiply *all* the divisors used on the left side.
*   **Verification:** A quick check helps avoid errors. Confirm if the result (540) is divisible by each of the original numbers (12, 15, 20, 27).
    *   540 / 12 = 45
    *   540 / 15 = 36
    *   540 / 20 = 27
    *   540 / 27 = 20
    All are divisible.
*   **Alternative Method:** The [[01 - Number System/03.E03 - LCM using Prime Factorization Example.md]] (Explains the prime factorization method) is another valid approach, which involves finding prime factors of each number and taking the highest powers. Choose the method you find faster and less error-prone.
*   **Distractor Awareness:** Options like 180, 270, and 1080 are often related but incorrect. 180 is the LCM of 12, 15, 20 but ignores 27 (needs 3^3). 1080 is 2*540 (a common multiple, not the least). Ensure you complete the division process fully.
