# Example: Decimal to Binary Conversion

**Tags:** #quant #number_system #base_conversion #example #decimal_to_binary

**Concept:** [[06 - Base Conversions]]

---

**Problem:**
Convert the decimal number 25₁₀ to its binary equivalent (base-2).

**Method Applied:**
Repeated division by the target base (2), recording remainders. Read remainders bottom-up.

**Solution:**

| Division        | Quotient | Remainder |
| :-------------- | :------- | :-------- |
| 25 ÷ 2          | 12       | **1**     | <--- LSB (Least Significant Bit)
| 12 ÷ 2          | 6        | **0**     |
| 6 ÷ 2           | 3        | **0**     |
| 3 ÷ 2           | 1        | **1**     |
| 1 ÷ 2           | 0        | **1**     | <--- MSB (Most Significant Bit)

Read the remainders from bottom to top: 11001.

**Conclusion:**
The decimal number 25₁₀ is equivalent to **11001₂** in binary.

**Shortcut/Exam Tip:**
The repeated division method is the standard and most reliable way to convert from decimal to any other base. Ensure you read the remainders in the correct (reverse) order.
