# Example: Decimal to Octal Conversion

**Tags:** #quant #number_system #base_conversion #example #decimal_to_octal

**Concept:** [[06 - Base Conversions]]

---

**Problem:**
Convert the decimal number 75₁₀ to its octal equivalent (base-8).

**Method Applied:**
Repeated division by the target base (8), recording remainders. Read remainders bottom-up.

**Solution:**

| Division        | Quotient | Remainder |
| :-------------- | :------- | :-------- |
| 75 ÷ 8          | 9        | **3**     | <--- Least Significant Digit
| 9 ÷ 8           | 1        | **1**     |
| 1 ÷ 8           | 0        | **1**     | <--- Most Significant Digit

Read the remainders from bottom to top: 113.

**Conclusion:**
The decimal number 75₁₀ is equivalent to **113₈** in octal.

**Shortcut/Exam Tip:**
Same reliable method as Decimal-to-Binary, just using 8 as the divisor. Remember octal digits only go from 0 to 7.
