# Finding Sum of Factors Example

**Problem:** What is the sum of all distinct positive factors (divisors) of the number 240?

(A) 600
(B) 744
(C) 720
(D) 120

## Solution:

To find the sum of all factors of 240, we follow the method outlined in the parent concept [[01 - Number System/02 - Factors and Multiples.md]] (Provides the formula for the sum of factors based on prime factorization).

Step 1: Find the prime factorization of 240.
   240 = 24 * 10
   240 = (8 * 3) * (2 * 5)
   240 = (2^3 * 3^1) * (2^1 * 5^1)
   Combining the prime factors:
   240 = 2^(3+1) * 3^1 * 5^1
   240 = 2^4 * 3^1 * 5^1

Step 2: Identify the distinct prime factors and their powers.
   The prime factors are 2, 3, and 5.
   Their respective powers are a=4, b=1, c=1.

Step 3: Apply the formula for the sum of factors:
   `Sum = (1 + p1 + p1^2 + ... + p1^a) * (1 + p2 + ... + p2^b) * (1 + p3 + ... + p3^c)`
   Sum = (1 + 2^1 + 2^2 + 2^3 + 2^4) * (1 + 3^1) * (1 + 5^1)

Step 4: Calculate the sum of powers for each prime factor.
   Sum for prime 2: (1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16) = 31
   Sum for prime 3: (1 + 3) = 4
   Sum for prime 5: (1 + 5) = 6

Step 5: Multiply these sums together.
   Total Sum = 31 * 4 * 6
   Total Sum = 31 * 24
   Total Sum = 744

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Geometric Progression Formula:** For larger powers, calculating the sum `(1 + p + p^2 + ... + p^a)` can be quicker using the GP sum formula: `Sum = (p^(a+1) - 1) / (p - 1)`.
    *   For 2^4: Sum = (2^(4+1) - 1) / (2 - 1) = (2^5 - 1) / 1 = 32 - 1 = 31.
    *   For 3^1: Sum = (3^(1+1) - 1) / (3 - 1) = (3^2 - 1) / 2 = (9 - 1) / 2 = 8 / 2 = 4.
    *   For 5^1: Sum = (5^(1+1) - 1) / (5 - 1) = (5^2 - 1) / 4 = (25 - 1) / 4 = 24 / 4 = 6.
    Total Sum = 31 * 4 * 6 = 744. This method is less prone to arithmetic errors when summing many terms.
*   **Include `p^0`:** A common error is forgetting the `p^0 = 1` term in each bracket. Ensure the sum for `p^a` includes `a+1` terms, starting from 1.
*   **Distractor Logic:**
    *   (A) 600: Might result from calculation errors or perhaps missing a term (e.g., forgetting the `+1` for a power).
    *   (C) 720: A familiar number (like 3 * 240), might be chosen if unsure. It's also 240 * 3.
    *   (D) 120: Could be half of 240, or mistakenly related to the number of factors (which is (4+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 5*2*2 = 20). Be careful not to confuse the sum calculation with the count calculation.
