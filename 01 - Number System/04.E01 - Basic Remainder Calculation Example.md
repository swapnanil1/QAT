# Basic Remainder Calculation Example

**Problem:**

What is the remainder when 4567 is divided by 23?

(A) 8
(B) 12
(C) 13
(D) 14

## Solution:

This problem requires finding the remainder using basic long division, a fundamental concept related to the [[01 - Number System/04 - Remainder Theorem.md]] (Which formalizes the idea that Dividend = Divisor * Quotient + Remainder).

**Step 1: Perform Long Division.**
Divide 4567 (Dividend) by 23 (Divisor).
   198   <-- Quotient
```
23 | 4567  
-23  
---  
226  
-207 (23 * 9 = 207)  
----  
197  
-184 (23 * 8 = 184)  
----  
13 <-- Remainder
```

**Step 2: Detailed Division Steps:**
- Divide 45 by 23. Quotient is 1, Remainder is 45 - (1 * 23) = 22.
- Bring down the next digit (6) to form 226.
- Divide 226 by 23. Estimate: 23 * 10 = 230 (too high), try 23 * 9 = 207. Quotient is 9, Remainder is 226 - 207 = 19.
- Bring down the next digit (7) to form 197.
- Divide 197 by 23. Estimate: 23 * 8 = 184. Quotient is 8, Remainder is 197 - 184 = 13.

**Step 3: Identify the final remainder.**
The final remainder obtained after the division process is 13.

This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Standard Division:** For moderately sized numbers like this, direct long division is often the most straightforward and reliable method. Avoid looking for complex theorems when basic arithmetic suffices.
*   **Estimation:** Practice estimating the quotient digits quickly. For 226 / 23, think "approx 230 / 23 = 10", so try 9. For 197 / 23, think "approx 200 / 20 = 10" or "approx 200 / 25 = 8", so try 8 or 9.
*   **Verification:** Check your answer using the Remainder Theorem formula: `Dividend = Divisor * Quotient + Remainder`.
 Here: `23 * 198 + 13 = 4554 + 13 = 4567`. The calculation matches the original dividend, confirming the remainder is correct.
*   **Distractor Awareness:** Options often include the last digit of the quotient (8, option A), or numbers close to the correct remainder resulting from minor calculation errors (12 or 14, options B and D). Double-check the final subtraction.
