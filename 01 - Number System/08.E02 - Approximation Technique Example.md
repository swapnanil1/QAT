# Approximation Technique Example

**Problem:**

What is the approximate value that should come in place of the question mark (?) in the following equation?
`(419.75 ÷ 19.98) + (25.03% of 399.88) - √64.1 = ?`

(A) 95
(B) 113
(C) 135
(D) 150

## Solution:

The problem asks for an approximate value, indicating that we should round the numbers to make calculations easier, as described in [[01 - Number System/08 - Simplification and Approximation.md]] (Explains approximation techniques).

**Step 1: Approximate each term in the expression.**
   - `419.75 ÷ 19.98`: Round 419.75 to 420 and 19.98 to 20.
     Approximate value ≈ 420 ÷ 20
   - `25.03% of 399.88`: Round 25.03% to 25% and 399.88 to 400.
     Approximate value ≈ 25% of 400 (Recall 25% = 1/4, see [[02 - Percentages/01 - Basic Concepts and Conversions.md]] (Provides percentage-fraction equivalents)).
   - `√64.1`: Round 64.1 to 64, which is a perfect square.
     Approximate value ≈ √64

**Step 2: Calculate the approximate values of the terms.**
   - `420 ÷ 20 = 21`
   - `25% of 400 = (1/4) * 400 = 100`
   - `√64 = 8`

**Step 3: Substitute the approximate values back into the expression.**
   The expression becomes approximately: `21 + 100 - 8`

**Step 4: Calculate the final approximate value.**
   `21 + 100 - 8 = 121 - 8 = 113`

The approximate value of the expression is 113.

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Identify Keywords:** Look for words like "approximate", "nearly", "closest to" which signal that you should round off the numbers.
*   **Strategic Rounding:** Round numbers to the nearest integers or convenient values (like multiples of 10 or perfect squares) that simplify the arithmetic operations (division, percentage, root calculation).
*   **Check Options:** Observe the gap between the options. If they are widely spread (like here: 95, 113, 135, 150), approximation is safer and faster. If options are very close, more careful calculation or less aggressive rounding might be needed.
*   **Percentage Conversion:** Convert percentages to simple fractions (25% = 1/4, 50% = 1/2, 75% = 3/4, 10% = 1/10, etc.) for quick calculation.
*   **Order of Operations:** Even in approximation, follow the VBODMAS rule (Division/Multiplication/Percentage/Roots before Addition/Subtraction).
