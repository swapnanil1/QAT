# Example: Divisibility by 72 (Composite Number)

**Tags:** #quant #number_system #divisibility #example #composite_divisibility #divisibility_by_72

**Concept:** [[01 - Divisibility Rules]]

---

**Problem:**
Is the number 15768 divisible by 72?

**Rule Applied:**
To check divisibility by a composite number (72), break it into co-prime factors and check divisibility by each factor.
Co-prime factors of 72 are 8 and 9 (since HCF(8, 9) = 1).
The number must be divisible by BOTH 8 AND 9.

**Solution:**

**Step 1: Check divisibility by 8**
*   Rule: Check the last three digits.
*   Last three digits of 15768 are 768.
*   Check if 768 is divisible by 8:
    *   76 / 8 = 9 remainder 4.
    *   Bring down 8, making it 48.
    *   48 / 8 = 6.
*   Yes, 768 is divisible by 8.

**Step 2: Check divisibility by 9**
*   Rule: Check if the sum of digits is divisible by 9.
*   Digits of 15768 are 1, 5, 7, 6, 8.
*   Sum of digits = 1 + 5 + 7 + 6 + 8 = 27.
*   Check if 27 is divisible by 9:
    *   27 / 9 = 3.
*   Yes, 27 is divisible by 9.

**Conclusion:**
Since the number 15768 is divisible by **both** 8 and 9 (which are co-prime factors of 72), the number 15768 **is** divisible by 72.

**Shortcut/Exam Tip:**
Breaking down the divisor into co-prime factors and applying their individual (simpler) rules is the standard fast method for checking divisibility by composite numbers like 72, 36, 45, 88, etc. Always choose co-prime factors (e.g., for 36 use 4 & 9, not 6 & 6 or 3 & 12).
