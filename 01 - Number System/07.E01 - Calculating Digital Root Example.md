# Calculating Digital Root Example

**Problem:**

What is the digital root of the number 682194?

(A) 3
(B) 30
(C) 9
(D) 6

## Solution:

The digital root of a number is the single-digit value obtained by an iterative process of summing digits, using the result of each summation as the input to the next, until a single digit is reached. This process is explained in [[01 - Number System/07 - Digital Root (Casting Out Nines).md]] (Details the concept and calculation methods).

**Step 1: Sum the digits of the number.**
   Number = 682194
   Sum = 6 + 8 + 2 + 1 + 9 + 4 = 30

**Step 2: If the sum is not a single digit, repeat the process.**
   The sum obtained is 30, which is not a single digit.
   Sum the digits of 30:
   Sum = 3 + 0 = 3

**Step 3: Conclude the result.**
   The sum is now a single digit (3).
   Therefore, the digital root of 682194 is 3.

This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Casting Out Nines:** During the summation process, you can ignore any digit that is 9, or any combination of digits that add up to 9 (or a multiple of 9), as they do not affect the final digital root (unless the number itself is 0 or a multiple of 9, where the root is 9).
    *   Number: 682194
    *   Ignore 9: 6 + 8 + 2 + 1 + 4
    *   Combine digits adding to 9 (optional, e.g., 8+1=9): 6 + 2 + 4 (Ignoring 8 and 1)
    *   Sum remaining: 6 + 2 + 4 = 12
    *   Reduce 12: 1 + 2 = 3.
    This often makes the initial summation much simpler.
*   **Modulo 9 Relationship:** The digital root of a positive integer `N` is equivalent to `N mod 9`, with one exception: if `N mod 9 = 0`, the digital root is 9 (not 0).
    *   `682194 mod 9 = (6+8+2+1+9+4) mod 9 = 30 mod 9 = 3`. This confirms the digital root is 3.
*   **Range:** The digital root of any positive integer is always between 1 and 9, inclusive.
*   **Distractor Check:** Option (B) 30 is the intermediate sum before the final reduction. Option (C) 9 is the digital root for numbers divisible by 9. Option (D) 6 is the first digit of the number. Ensure you reduce the sum completely to a single digit.
