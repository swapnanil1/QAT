# Divisibility by 11 Example

**Problem:** If the 6-digit number `9y8071` (where `y` is a single digit) is divisible by 11, what is the value of `y + 3`?

(A) 1
(B) 4
(C) 7
(D) 12

## Solution:

This problem requires applying the divisibility rule for 11, as described in the parent concept [[01 - Number System/01 - Divisibility Rules.md]] (States the rule based on the difference between sums of digits at odd and even places).

Step 1: Identify the digits at odd and even places, starting from the right (Units place is 1st position).
   Number: `9 y 8 0 7 1`
   Positions: 6 5 4 3 2 1

   Digits at odd places (1st, 3rd, 5th): 1, 0, y
   Digits at even places (2nd, 4th, 6th): 7, 8, 9

Step 2: Calculate the sum of digits at odd places and even places.
   Sum (Odd) = 1 + 0 + y = `1 + y`
   Sum (Even) = 7 + 8 + 9 = 24

Step 3: Find the difference between these sums.
   Difference = | (Sum Odd) - (Sum Even) | = | `(1 + y)` - 24 | = | `y - 23` |

Step 4: Apply the divisibility rule for 11.
   The difference (`y - 23`) must be either 0 or a multiple of 11.
   Since `y` is a single digit (0 to 9), the value of `y - 23` must be in the range [0 - 23, 9 - 23], which is [-23, -14].

Step 5: Find the multiple of 11 within the calculated range [-23, -14].
   The only multiple of 11 in this range is -22.

Step 6: Solve for `y`.
   `y - 23 = -22`
   `y = -22 + 23`
   `y = 1`

Step 7: Calculate the value required by the problem, which is `y + 3`.
   Required Value = `y + 3 = 1 + 3 = 4`.

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Alternating Signs:** Instead of summing odd/even places separately, you can alternate signs starting from the right: `+1 - 7 + 0 - 8 + y - 9`. The result must be 0 or a multiple of 11.
    Calculation: `1 - 7 + 0 - 8 + y - 9 = y - 23`. This leads to the same equation `y - 23 = -22` or `y - 23 = 0, +/- 11, +/- 22, ...`
*   **Range Check:** The key step often missed is checking the possible range of the difference based on the unknown digit (`y`). This significantly limits the possible multiples of 11 you need to consider (here, only -22 worked).
*   **Final Answer:** Always double-check what the question asks for. Here it's `y + 3`, not just `y`. A common mistake is to solve for `y` and select that value (Option A) if available.
*   **Distractor Logic:** Option (A) is the value of `y`. Options (C) and (D) might arise from calculation errors in the sums or incorrectly choosing a different multiple of 11 (like -11 or 0).
