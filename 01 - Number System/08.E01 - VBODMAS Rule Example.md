# VBODMAS Rule Example

**Problem:**

What is the value of the expression: `48 ÷ 12 * {4 + (15 - 3 * 2)}`?

(A) 52
(B) 4
(C) 0
(D) 48

## Solution:

This problem requires simplifying the given expression using the VBODMAS rule, which dictates the order of operations, as explained in [[01 - Number System/08 - Simplification and Approximation.md]] (Details the VBODMAS sequence).

**Step 1: Identify the parts of the expression and apply VBODMAS.**
Expression: `48 ÷ 12 * {4 + (15 - 3 * 2)}`
Order: Brackets (innermost first), Multiplication/Division (left-to-right), Addition/Subtraction (left-to-right).

**Step 2: Solve the innermost bracket `(15 - 3 * 2)`.**
   - Inside this bracket, multiplication comes before subtraction (VBODMAS).
   - Calculate `3 * 2 = 6`.
   - Now the bracket is `(15 - 6)`.
   - Calculate `15 - 6 = 9`.
   - The expression becomes: `48 ÷ 12 * {4 + 9}`.

**Step 3: Solve the curly bracket `{4 + 9}`.**
   - Calculate `4 + 9 = 13`.
   - The expression becomes: `48 ÷ 12 * 13`.

**Step 4: Perform Division and Multiplication from left to right.**
   - The operations remaining are Division (÷) and Multiplication (*). They have equal precedence, so we proceed from left to right.
   - First, perform the division: `48 ÷ 12 = 4`.
   - The expression becomes: `4 * 13`.
   - Now, perform the multiplication: `4 * 13 = 52`.

**Step 5: Final Result.**
The value of the expression is 52.

This matches option (A).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Left-to-Right for Equal Precedence:** The most common error in VBODMAS problems involving Division/Multiplication or Addition/Subtraction is forgetting to perform them from left to right when they appear together. In Step 4, `48 ÷ 12 * 13`, doing multiplication first (12 * 13 = 156, then 48 ÷ 156) would give a wrong answer. Always go left-to-right for `/` and `*`, and similarly for `+` and `-`.
*   **Bracket Priority:** Always resolve brackets starting from the innermost ones (`()`), then (`{}`), then (`[]`). Operations inside brackets follow their own VBODMAS rules.
*   **'Of' Operation:** Remember that 'Of' means multiplication but has higher precedence than standard Division/Multiplication. Handle 'Of' after brackets but before D/M. (Not present in this example).
*   **Write Intermediate Steps:** In exams, briefly writing down the result after each major step (like solving a bracket) can prevent confusion and make error checking easier if needed.
*   **Distractor Check:** Option (B) 4 comes from just doing `48 ÷ 12`. Option (D) 48 is the starting number. Option (C) 0 might occur from severe calculation errors.
