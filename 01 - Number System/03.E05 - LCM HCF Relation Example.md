# LCM HCF Relation Example

**Problem:**

The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 13 and 1989 respectively. If one of the numbers is 117, what is the other number?

(A) 199
(B) 221
(C) 234
(D) 153

## Solution:

This problem uses the fundamental relationship between the HCF, LCM, and the product of two numbers, as explained in the parent concept [[01 - Number System/03 - HCF and LCM.md]] (Provides the formula `HCF * LCM = Product of Numbers`).

**Step 1: State the relationship formula for two numbers.**
For any two positive integers, let the numbers be N1 and N2.
`HCF(N1, N2) * LCM(N1, N2) = N1 * N2`

**Step 2: Substitute the given values into the formula.**
We are given:
HCF = 13
LCM = 1989
One number (let N1) = 117
Let the other number be N2.

Substituting these values:
`13 * 1989 = 117 * N2`

**Step 3: Solve for the unknown number (N2).**
Rearrange the equation to find N2:
`N2 = (13 * 1989) / 117`

We can simplify the calculation by noticing that 117 is divisible by 13:
117 / 13 = 9.

So, we can cancel out 13 from the numerator and denominator (by dividing 117 by 13):
`N2 = (1 * 1989) / 9`

Now, divide 1989 by 9. (Check divisibility by 9: sum of digits 1+9+8+9 = 27, which is divisible by 9).
1989 / 9 = 221.

So, N2 = 221.

**Step 4: Conclude the answer.**
The other number is 221.

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Formula Application:** This type of problem is a direct application of the `HCF * LCM = N1 * N2` formula. Recognize it immediately.
*   **Simplify Before Multiplying:** Avoid calculating the large product `13 * 1989`. Look for cancellations first. Dividing 117 by the HCF (13) simplified the calculation significantly. `N2 = (HCF * LCM) / N1`. Since N1 must be divisible by the HCF, `N1 / HCF` will be an integer. So, `N2 = LCM / (N1 / HCF)`. Here, `N2 = 1989 / (117 / 13) = 1989 / 9 = 221`.
*   **Two Numbers Only:** Crucially remember that this formula (`HCF * LCM = Product`) works *only* for two numbers. It does not apply directly to three or more numbers.
*   **Verification (Optional):** Quickly check if HCF(117, 221) is indeed 13 and LCM(117, 221) is 1989.
    *   117 = 9 * 13 = 3^2 * 13
    *   221 = 13 * 17
    *   HCF = 13 (common factor with lowest power)
    *   LCM = 3^2 * 13 * 17 = 9 * 13 * 17 = 117 * 17 = 1989. The numbers fit the criteria.
*   **Distractor Check:** Option (A) 199 is a prime number close to 221. Option (C) 234 is 2 * 117. Option (D) 153 = 9 * 17, related to factors involved but incorrect.
