# GP Term and Sum Example

**Problem:**

Find the 6th term of the geometric progression (GP): 2, 6, 18, 54, ...

(A) 162
(B) 486
(C) 729
(D) 1458

## Solution:

This problem requires finding a specific term (the nth term) of a given Geometric Progression using the standard formulas discussed in [[18 - Miscellaneous (Optional)/02 - Series and Progressions (AP, GP).md|Series and Progressions (AP, GP)]].

**Step 1:** Identify the first term (a), the common ratio (r), and the term number (n).
   - First term, `a = 2`.
   - Common ratio, `r = a_2 / a_1 = 6 / 2 = 3`. (Check: `18 / 6 = 3`, `54 / 18 = 3`).
   - Term number, `n = 6`.

**Step 2:** Select the appropriate formula for the nth term of a GP.
   The formula is `a_n = a * r^(n-1)`.

**Step 3:** Substitute the identified values into the formula.
   `a_6 = 2 * 3^(6-1)`
   `a_6 = 2 * 3^5`

**Step 4:** Perform the calculation. Calculate `3^5` first.
   `3^1 = 3`
   `3^2 = 9`
   `3^3 = 27`
   `3^4 = 81`
   `3^5 = 243`
   Now substitute back:
   `a_6 = 2 * 243`
   `a_6 = 486`

The 6th term of the given GP is 486.

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Quick Identification:** Confirm it's a GP by checking for a constant ratio between consecutive terms. Quickly identify `a` (first term) and `r` (ratio).
*   **Formula Accuracy:** Use the correct nth term formula `a_n = a * r^(n-1)`. Don't confuse it with AP formulas or GP sum formulas.
*   **`n-1` Exponent Pitfall:** The exponent is `n-1`, not `n`. Here, `n-1 = 6-1 = 5`. Using `n=6` as the exponent would lead to `2 * 3^6 = 2 * 729 = 1458`, which is the 7th term and matches distractor (D).
*   **Power Calculation:** Calculate the power term `r^(n-1)` carefully. For small powers, manual multiplication is often reliable. `3^5 = 3 * 3 * 3 * 3 * 3 = 9 * 9 * 3 = 81 * 3 = 243`.
*   **Final Multiplication:** Remember to multiply by the first term `a` after calculating the power. Forgetting this step might lead to `3^5 = 243` as an answer, or confusing `3^6 = 729` (distractor C).
*   **Distractor Check:** Option (A) 162 is `2 * 3^4`, which is the 5th term (`n-1=4`). Option (C) 729 is `3^6`, potentially forgetting `a` or using `n` as the exponent. Option (D) 1458 is `2 * 3^6`, the 7th term (using `n` as the exponent).
