# Calendar Day Calculation Example

**Problem:**

What was the day of the week on 15th August 1947?

(A) Thursday
(B) Friday
(C) Saturday
(D) Sunday

## Solution:

This problem requires finding the day of the week for a specific date using the concept of odd days, as detailed in [[18 - Miscellaneous (Optional)/04 - Clocks and Calendars.md|Clocks and Calendars]]. We calculate the total number of odd days from a reference point (like year 0) up to the given date.

**Step 1:** Calculate odd days for the period until the end of the year 1946.
   We can break this into centuries and remaining years.
   - Odd days in 1600 years = 0 (since 400 year cycle has 0 odd days).
   - Odd days in the next 300 years (from 1601 to 1900) = 1 (since 100 years = 5 odd days, 200 = 3, 300 = 1).
   - Consider the years from 1901 to 1946 (46 years). We need to find the number of leap years and ordinary years in this period.
     - Leap years are divisible by 4. Leap years are: 1904, 1908, ..., 1944.
     - Number of leap years = (Last Leap Year - First Leap Year) / 4 + 1 = (1944 - 1904) / 4 + 1 = 40 / 4 + 1 = 10 + 1 = 11 leap years. Check using [[01 - Number System/01 - Divisibility Rules.md|divisibility rules]].
     - Number of ordinary years = Total years - Leap years = 46 - 11 = 35 ordinary years.
     - Odd days in these 46 years = (35 * 1) + (11 * 2) = 35 + 22 = 57 days.
     - 57 days = 8 weeks + 1 odd day (since 57 mod 7 = 1).
   - Total odd days up to Dec 31, 1946 = Odd days(1600) + Odd days(300) + Odd days(46 years) = 0 + 1 + 1 = 2 odd days.

**Step 2:** Calculate odd days for the period from 1st January 1947 to 15th August 1947.
   - 1947 is not a leap year (not divisible by 4). So, February has 28 days (0 odd days).
   - Odd days per month: Jan(31 days=3), Feb(28=0), Mar(31=3), Apr(30=2), May(31=3), Jun(30=2), Jul(31=3).
   - Days in August = 15.
   - Total days = 31(Jan) + 28(Feb) + 31(Mar) + 30(Apr) + 31(May) + 30(Jun) + 31(Jul) + 15(Aug)
   - Total days = 227 days.
   - Alternatively, sum odd days: 3(Jan) + 0(Feb) + 3(Mar) + 2(Apr) + 3(May) + 2(Jun) + 3(Jul) + 15(Aug) = 31 odd days (treating the 15 days directly).
   - Total odd days in this period = 227 mod 7 = 3 odd days (since 227 = 32 * 7 + 3). Or, 31 mod 7 = 3 odd days.

**Step 3:** Calculate the total odd days up to 15th August 1947.
   Total Odd Days = Odd days (up to 1946) + Odd days (in 1947 until Aug 15)
   Total Odd Days = 2 + 3 = 5 odd days.

**Step 4:** Determine the day of the week from the total odd days.
   Assuming Day 0 = Sunday, Day 1 = Monday, ..., Day 5 = Friday, Day 6 = Saturday.
   Since the total number of odd days is 5, the day was Friday.

This matches option (B).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Odd Day Calculation:** The core strategy is accumulating odd days. Breaking it down into centuries, completed years, and months within the target year is systematic.
*   **Leap Year Rules:** Remember the rule: Divisible by 4, EXCEPT if divisible by 100 UNLESS divisible by 400. Checking 1900 (not leap) vs 2000 (leap) is crucial for century calculations. 1947 is not divisible by 4, so it's ordinary.
*   **Month Odd Days:** Memorize or quickly derive odd days for months (31 days = 3 odd; 30 days = 2 odd; 28 days = 0 odd; 29 days = 1 odd).
*   **Day Code Consistency:** Stick to a consistent day code (e.g., 0=Sun or 0=Mon). Here we used 0=Sun, 1=Mon, ..., 5=Fri.
*   **Common Errors:** Miscounting leap years within a range, arithmetic errors when summing odd days, forgetting the century year leap rule, or using an inconsistent day code map.
*   **Distractor Check:** Options A, C, D represent days immediately before or after Friday, which could result from being off by one in the odd day calculation – a very common mistake.
