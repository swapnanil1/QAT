# Clock Angle Calculation Example

**Problem:**

What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock when the time is 3:40 PM?

(A) 120°
(B) 125°
(C) 130°
(D) 140°

## Solution:

This problem requires calculating the angle between the clock hands at a specific time using the formula derived from their relative speeds, as discussed in [[18 - Miscellaneous (Optional)/04 - Clocks and Calendars.md|Clocks and Calendars]].

**Step 1:** Identify the hour (H) and minute (M) values.
   H = 3
   M = 40

**Step 2:** Apply the formula for the angle (θ) between the hour hand and minute hand:
   `θ = |(11/2)M - 30H|`

**Step 3:** Substitute the values of H and M into the formula.
   `θ = |(11/2) * 40 - 30 * 3|`

**Step 4:** Perform the calculation.
   `θ = |11 * (40/2) - 90|`
   `θ = |11 * 20 - 90|`
   `θ = |220 - 90|`
   `θ = |130|`
   `θ = 130°`

The angle between the hands is 130°. Since this is less than 180°, it is the required smaller angle.

This matches option (C).

## Shortcut/Tip

*   **Direct Formula:** The formula `θ = |(11/2)M - 30H|` is the most direct method. Memorize it.
*   **Avoid Decimal Hours:** Do not convert the hour to a decimal like 3.66 for 3:40 when using this formula. The formula correctly accounts for the hour hand's movement *during* the hour based on the minute value M. Using `H=3` is correct.
*   **Reflex Angle Check:** The formula gives the absolute difference. If the result `θ` were greater than 180°, the smaller angle would be `360° - θ`. In this case, 130° is less than 180°, so it's the answer.
*   **Mental Estimation:** At 3:00, the angle is 90°. At 3:40, the minute hand is at 8 (240° from 12) and the hour hand has moved 2/3rds of the way from 3 to 4. The hour hand position is `3*30 + 40*0.5 = 90 + 20 = 110°` from 12. The difference is `240° - 110° = 130°`. This confirms the formula result.
*   **Distractor Check:** Option (A) 120° might result from rounding or slight miscalculation. Option (D) 140° could arise from errors in calculating the hour hand's intermediate position. Option (B) 125° is another plausible calculation error.
